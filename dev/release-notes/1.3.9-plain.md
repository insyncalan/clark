# clark 1.3.9, Mac

This is a polish release on 1.3. It is a full release.

## What is new

### Classic

- The profile menu (the circle) lists Preview, Export to Excel, Publish…, Published reports, Settings, then Appearance, then Help. [1281]
- Appearance is one line: Light, Dark and System side by side in a pill, the chosen one marked. The version ("clark ver." and its number) is on the right of the Help line. [1281]
- "Export to Word" is no longer in the profile menu. It is in Settings › Data and in the Publish box. [1281]
- Priority icons in the peek and the pickers: Urgent is maroon, High is red, Medium, Low and no priority are grey. For a risk or an issue, Medium is amber and Low is green. Actions keep Medium and Low grey. The Cards theme is unchanged. [1390]
- The High priority chip on the Work items page and the Dashboard list is a new pale red (background F4DEDB, text B3392F). The High cell of the rating matrix changes with it. Dark keeps its colours. [1390]
- Dashboard, View: a list set beside another (half width) now has the same four pickers as a full-width list: urgency, date, owner and status. Still not in Edit, not in a published file, and not in Cards. A click on the description opens the peek in edit. [1287]
- Dashboard, View: in the Risks, Actions, Decisions and Work items lists you can change urgency, date, owner and status in place with the same pickers as in Edit. A pick is saved at once with a toast and Undo. The description is never editable there; a click on it opens the peek in edit. An empty owner or date says "none". A published file and the client report stay read only. [1287]
- New Quote template: up to 6 quotes, each with the words, a name and a role. Choose Left or Centre and 2 or 3 quotes in a row; a half-width section and a window 900 px wide or narrower show them one under the other. [1271]
- Quote: "Tile" and "Heading" are two switches; turn either off, or both, and the quote sits directly on the page. In Edit the words and the name and role are edited in place, with Add and Remove (Add is off at 6; "Up to 6 quotes (n of 6)"). An empty quote is left out of View and the published file. [1271]
- The library card says Holds: Quote, Said by, Role. A section with the older one-quote shape keeps its text and is still published. Help (Section templates) describes the Quote section. [1271]
- Board: the Group by and Move to menus have lines with padding 7px 10px and 10px between the icon and the words; the tick stays to the right of the word, with no space for it before the icon. The two heads are in capitals ("Group by", "Move <card id> to"). [1273]
- Board: the card's Move to menu head reads "Move <card id> to" in Classic (for example the id of the card); Cards keeps "Move to <group>". The use case Group by menu is not changed. [1273]
- The template "Full width image" is now called "Image" and the picker offers it at full and half width. The old "Image with caption" entry is gone from the picker; sections made with it still open. Description: "One image, framed as you choose, with an optional caption." [1223]
- At half width the Image template works in Edit and View (and in the Word report); changing from full to half and back keeps the picture. Help words match the new name. [1223]
- The note under the picture still says "printed full width" at half width (no new word was ruled). [1223]
- Work items page: the priority pills now take the same colours as the priority pills in the list, in light and dark. [1353]
- Peek: the Source line under the fields shows the source only; the " by <name>" at its end is gone, in Edit and in View. The Source help line says the same. The "Shown to the client" label and the "Show history" row stay as they were. [1258]
- Image gallery: new galleries are stored in a new form (version 2) that keeps for each picture a framed copy and the original, with its focus point, crop, caption and "Describe the image" words. A new gallery starts at 4:3 and 3 across at full width; the page shows 2 across at half width and at 900 px. Only the framed copies are published. [1233]
- Old galleries stay exactly as they were (checked, saved and published the same; adding a picture does not change them). A gallery picture may be up to 10 MB. [1233]
- Section templates are named by what they do. In "Add a section" and as the Help row heading: Text with image (was Text and Image Side by Side), Numbers (was Key figures, four and three; both cards are named Numbers for now), Picture grid, Item cards, three / four (was RAID cards), Work items, Files and links, Must-see note, Who to ask, Next steps, Coming dates, Web page card, One file, "Where we are, what’s next", Gantt table, Use cases, Documents. [1262]
- A new section still starts with its old title (for example "Key figures", "Callout", "Table"). Report titles, Word, Excel and published headings are not changed. The message after adding one follows the card name, for example "Use cases added." [1262]
- The kind "Figures" in Add a section is now "Numbers". Help search for "keys" finds 9 entries (was 11). [1262]
- "Image with caption" and "Full width image" keep their names. [1262]

### Settings

- Ask clark settings, All projects tab: "Changes and safety" now comes before "Usage", so Usage is last. [1351]
- Settings, project table (Classic): the column heads are weight 600 in the muted text colour with padding 6px 10px 6px 0, and each body cell has a thin bottom line (1px, the soft line colour). The Cards look is not changed. [1317]
- Settings, All projects tab (Classic): a row "Asking for new projects" with a switch and the line "A project can still turn asking off in its own tab." It changes no project that exists. [1503]
- Settings, a project's tab (Classic): "Asking is on for <code>." (or "off") with a "Turn off asking" / "Turn on asking" button for that project only, and the line "The default for new projects is set under All projects." [1503]
- The computer-wide switch now reads "Asking is on for all projects." with "Turn off asking for all projects" / "Turn on asking for all projects" (Cards keeps its old words). A project whose own asking is off reads as off in the ask panel before anything is sent. A change that cannot be saved says so, and a saved change has an Undo. [1503]
- Settings, Update page rows (Classic): the ready card has "Released 18-11-2026 · 38 MB." (date and size, as known), then the notes, then "Your projects, settings and published reports are kept." [1327]
- A "Last checked" row: "Today 11:42. clark looks quietly when it starts and when you choose Check now. It never installs by itself." with the button "Check now" (an older day shows the date; before the first check "Not checked yet."). [1327]
- A "Use a file instead" row: "For a computer with no internet. Review a clark download you already have." with "Review" and "Choose zip…". After an update: "Updated to clark 1.3.2" with "clark restarted and kept everything. If something looks wrong you can go back to 1.3.1." and Undo. [1327]
- When nothing is new: "You have the latest version (1.3.1). Last checked today 11:42." Offline: "clark could not reach the releases page. Check your connection, or use a file you downloaded." with "Try again". A failed check: "The download failed its check" with "Download again". [1327]

### Ask clark

- Escape in the ask panel closes the slash menu first and leaves the panel open (the panel closed together with the menu before). [1277]
- Ask clark: a project bigger than the model can take is no longer refused. Up to 1,200,000 characters of passages (about 300,000 tokens) go in one request, 60 passages at most, 4,000 characters each; the least relevant passages are left out. [1292]
- When passages are left out the answer shows "Left out to keep this question under the size limit: N passages." (the count adds the request cut and the project cut). The question is refused only when not even one passage fits. [1292]
- The project's own data may now take half of the ask clark budget (600,000 characters of 1,200,000); the source folders get the rest. [1508]

### Help

- Help says where Export to Word is now: "Settings › Data › Export to Word…". Three pictures (Export to Excel menu, Publish menu, Arrange menu) show the new menu. [1281]
Cards: the menu is unchanged. [1281]
- Two Help lines no longer say "Turn off asking". [1503]
- Help says that Clear (Publishing, client and local folder) shows only once a folder is chosen; Remove (Brands) only on a brand you added; Undo (Brands) only when there is a change to put back. [1205]

### Word report

- The Plan and Gantt pictures: a lane name of 38 characters or fewer wraps onto two lines without dots (at a space when there is one); a longer name keeps ".." at the end of line 2. A two-line lane is taller (32 px); other lanes keep one-line height. [1322]

### Under the hood

- Tests only: the bold 13 px "Latest update" label and the grey "Shown to the client" value [1285]; no animation on the Classic Draft sheet with reduced motion [1347]; the Dashboard list with "Most rows 50" and 51 items (50 shown, "Show all items" gives 51, "Show only 50" back to 50) [1359].
- The Edit page for the gallery is not changed in this release; the Edit redesign of row 1233 is not built yet (donecheck 1233 PARTIAL, 23-11 ~20:50). [1233]
- The camera-details note reads "That image still carries details from the camera, such as where it was taken. Add it again with ..., which removes them." (it said "That picture ..."). [1233]
- Three test fixes for the macOS runs (Help focus test, Peek slide test, the Milestones column key test) and a new test that bans sleep waits in four test files. [1277]
- The empty-box text of the Latest update block ("What changed? Add the latest update…") is now written once and used by the work item Peek and the milestone Peek. Same words, no change you see. New tests pin the bold "Latest update" label and the grey "Shown to the client" value. [1548]
- Published file, top bar (part 1 of 5): above 900 px the pages sit on the lower line at the same right edge as in the editor, with the editor's side padding and gap. Three or four pages stay right-aligned. The "As at" date moves into the room reserved for the editor's buttons. Unselected tabs are 92 percent white so the text stays readable on the picture. More parts follow. [1543]
- Image gallery: tests added that pin the data shape (mutation survivors) and the published view (2 across at half width and at 900 px, 3 across at full width). The Edit page code has only a comment added. Nothing a person sees changes. [1233]
- Settings, Projects overview, data side only (the screen follows in a later PR): the server can now give one read-only row for each project (Asking, Brand, Read folders, Review days, Client folder, Last backup) and the count of rows that need attention (no client folder, no backup or a backup older than one day, asking on with no folders). Archived projects are left out. Nothing new is shown. [1299]
- Feedback tag (Classic): the dialog text is 15 px as in the mock-up (the page stays 14 px); the Copy and Mail buttons keep 14 px. The test now pins these sizes. [1334]
