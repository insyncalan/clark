# clark 1.3.2, Mac

This is the third build of the 1.3 line. It follows 1.3.1.

## What is new

### Classic

- The Classic Theme is much closer to its design. The page has a soft light-to-lighter background, in light and in dark.
- Headings are a little lighter in weight. Buttons and chosen segments have rounded corners, and a chosen segment is navy with white text. In dark it has a thin edge so you can see it. Breadcrumbs are plain, not in capitals.
- Main buttons are aqua pills with a soft glow. They lift a little on hover and sink a little when pressed.
- The Dashboard has no outer white card. Every other section sits on a white band. A Contents list on the left replaces the right-hand rail and shows from 1000 px wide. The At a glance strip is flat with dividers between the cells, the Milestones switch and scale are joined, and the project title stays on one line.
- Work items, Milestones, Use cases and Settings have a thin 2 px rule down the left side.
- Milestones no longer has the sentence under its title.
- On Work items, the dashed + sits at the far right of the saved-views row.
- The Work items page has an underlined filter line (red when the filter cannot be read), and the view tabs wrap on a narrow window.
- The Work items list in Edit follows the approved layout: the gear sits top right of the title row, then the saved views, then the filter. The + menu shows a letter tile for each kind (Risk, Action, Issue, Decision). Configure is tidied and lists the columns in a new order. Ids are bold and in the link colour, and priority pills have a border.
- The Use cases page has a plain breadcrumb, a search box with a magnifier, a framed list with a grey header strip, and the commitment as a quiet pill under the status. The Next steps count is plain text, not a round badge.

### Ask clark

- The panel keeps your chat and its answer when you move to another page. The ask button shows how many answers are ready.
- A new chat shows three plain lines on what ask clark reads, that nothing is saved until Apply and every change can be undone, and where the question goes. It has a link to the Asking card in Settings.
- When an answer comes with a draft, it shows "Draft ready" and opens a Draft sheet beside the panel. You can edit it, copy it, or add it to the report by replacing the text of a Text section or adding a new Text section. Each is one change, so one Undo. Closing the sheet leaves the report as it was.
- Typing / in the box opens a menu of saved questions: /check, /summary, /overdue, /risks, /milestones, /usecases, /history and /new.
- The quick bar has an "Ask ... in ask clark" row, first when your words read like a question.
- You can add files and pictures to the next question with the paperclip, by drop or by paste. A question can carry five files of up to 10 MB each.
- The status line says "Standing instructions on." when standing instructions are set.
- The ask clark page in Settings has two tabs, All projects and the open project. The open project's tab opens first and has a "Used from All projects" card with a Change button.
- On the All projects tab you can turn Claude and ChatGPT on or off, and press "Test the connection". Each model that is on is asked one short question, and nothing is kept.
- Standing instructions: one box for all projects and one for the open project, up to 4,000 characters each. They save when you leave the box, can be undone, and are sent above every question. Text that looks like a password or a key is refused.
- Folders ask clark may read can be set once for all projects. Each project shows them tagged "All projects", then its own folders, up to 20 in all.

### Settings

- The Settings page has a Connectors block, "Asking for all projects". See Known limits.

### Dashboard

- A tile you hide with the eye leaves Edit view of At a glance. An "Add a tile" menu lists hidden tiles and shows them again in one step, with one Undo. The Glance template menu lists them too.
- Project health fills half the row beside two tiles and three quarters beside one, above 1180 px wide. Three tiles keep the four-up row.
- A new project's default report has Risks and issues, Open actions and Decisions as Work items lists, each with its own saved filter. Risks and issues and Open actions show open items, most urgent or soonest due first, up to 50. Decisions shows made and pending decisions, up to 50. A report you already have keeps its own sections.

### Published file

- A file published from a project with a shown Work items list gets the Work items tab, even when the report has no Risks, Actions or Decisions section.

### Word

- A Work items list in Word shows an action's Latest update line under the item.

## What is changed

- The Plan summary section left as the untouched template ("Where we are", "What is next") is left out of the published report. If you typed anything in it, it is published.
- The Work items section's Edit no longer has the large "Choose from lists" panel. The filter box, the tabs and Configure set the same filter.
- The Priority column is a little wider, so the pill and its arrow always fit.
- With Cards, the Milestones and Use cases pages show the project name above the title as plain text. Cards' Milestones page no longer shows the "is agreed for ..." sentence. The board's scroll bars are hidden, though it still scrolls. Menus are wider with larger text. The Add button is 28 px high, with a dashed blue border that fills blue on hover or focus.
- The View | Edit pill and the V and E keys work again on a Dashboard with a Work items list, and when the Welcome card is showing. The Welcome card no longer takes the focus after you press V or E.

## Known limits

- Settings has a Connectors block (Asking for all projects) with On/Off switches. The switches save, but ask clark does not use them yet. All of them are Off.
- On the Classic Dashboard in Edit, a hidden section can shift which sections get the white band. The View and the published page are not affected.
- Slash commands: the built-in ones in the box work, but there is no page yet to edit, copy or add your own.
- Settings for a project that is not open is not available. The tabs are All projects and the open project.
