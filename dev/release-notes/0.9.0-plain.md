# clark 0.9.0 (Beta), Mac

Beta means the whole of clark is Beta until version 1.0.

Theme: tidy what 0.8.0 left over, and finish the pieces that were waiting for sign-off.

## What is new

- The bar above the page has a Week ending box in View as well as in Edit. With no week kept it shows the coming Friday and saves nothing until you press Enter on it; a typed day that is not a Friday keeps the old date and says which Friday it needs.
- The project name in the top bar is now always a menu. It lists each project with its number of open items and has Project settings, and it says "Switched to" the project when you change.
- Work items has a "Show archived" pill beside View that shows how many items are archived and remembers your choice for each project. Archived items show in a muted table; one opens in the side panel read only, with one Restore. Restore puts the item back and leaves the panel open on it, now editable.

## What is fixed

- Clearing a work item's client message now says so in plain words, instead of a server error, and nothing is saved.
- About you: saving your name before clark has finished reading what it already holds no longer blanks your email, and saving your email first no longer blanks your name.

## Behind the scenes

- The first step of the Claude sign-in work for ask clark: the models come from a fixed list and the sign-in check asks for a model only when one is chosen (nothing new to see on screen).
- The suggestion route in the AI menu builds its request in one place.
- A publish that replaces a link puts the old one back without a gap, with a link that is not followed, and a platform error is caught.
- Tests and checks only: the generated test pages are built in one shared helper, and the macOS rule in the checks has a follow-up.
