# clark 0.9.0 (Beta), Mac

Beta means the whole of clark is Beta until version 1.0.

Theme: bring more screens closer to the prototype, with the Work items table, the progress section, the Draft email and Settings first.

## What is new

- Work items: each item has a letter badge (A, R, I, D, S or P) in place of the Type column, each group is a card of its own, and a Client-visible column shows an eye for the items your client sees. In Edit the eye turns an item off or on, with a message and Undo. In View it is a mark only, and a published file has no eye column.
- Draft email: for a chase you can pick First chase, Second chase or Firmer chase. It starts on the tone clark would have chosen, and the line beside it says how often the items were chased before, for example "Chased 2 times before". Picking a tone writes a new draft. No tone word goes into the email unless you write it.
- The progress section is closer to the approved design. Overall is three tiles: Project health, Achieved ("of the whole project") and Planned ("by" a date). Below them the bar has a "Planned" label over its mark and a scale from 0% to 100%. The Achieved column of each line has a bar beside its figure.
- Settings: the row of chips is a bar of its own under the top bar. It stays in view as you scroll, never covers a card, and the chip of the card you are reading is marked.
- About you says what it saved, with Undo: "Name saved.", "Email saved.", "Name cleared." or "Name and email saved." Undo puts the earlier words back in the box and the menu.

## What is fixed

- A date picker opened low in the Work items table now sits above the messages at the bottom left, so its "No date" button can be pressed while a message shows.

## Behind the scenes

- Tests only: more tests for the settings chip bar, the About you messages, the progress section's tiles, the chase tone and the Work items table; the Help entries that are still to come are recorded in a file, with no change to what Help says.
