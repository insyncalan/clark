# clark 1.0.3 (Beta), Mac

This is the 1.0 build. It is still marked Beta because updating from inside clark is not switched on yet.

This version does not change how clark stores your data. Your project files open as they did in 1.0.2.

## What is new

- The project menu in the top bar now ends with New project... It opens the same form as Settings, Projects, and the new project opens on Work items. The menu opens on the project you are in.
- In Edit, Ctrl+J (Cmd+J on a Mac) opens ask clark from anywhere, including while you are typing in a field. The ask clark button's tooltip now reads "Open ask clark (/ or Ctrl or Cmd + J)".
- The two switches in Settings, Asking and Single-key shortcuts, now say what they were turned to ("ask clark is on", "Single-key shortcuts are off" and so on) in a message with Undo, which sets the switch back. The message goes when you leave the card.
- The eye on a work item can now be clicked in View as well as in Edit, on the Work items list and on the Use cases list. On a Work items board the card's eye is now a button too, with the same message and Undo.
- On Work items, when nothing matches the words you typed, the list says, for example, No open items match "crane". (or No items match, when All or Closed is chosen), offers Clear search, and adds a hint: "Plain words search titles. Press / to come back here."
- On a use case, a work item in the list and a linked milestone's name each open their peek beside the page; the page stays where it is. A linked milestone also shows its state, with the same word and mark as the Milestones page.
- The Client Excel workbook's sheets are now protected without a password, in the same way as the export's sheets. The ID column, the worked-out columns (Original, Shown state) and the other columns the export locks are locked; to change a locked cell, use Review, Unprotect Sheet.

## What is changed

- On Work items, a click on a row or a card now opens its peek and selects only that item; a double-click is no longer needed. Cmd or Ctrl+click and Shift+click still only select. Help says so.
- Help now says that the links to the Settings cards are a list down the left of the cards, and a row of chips under the title in a window 900 px wide or narrower.
- Messages (toasts) are now a rounded pill, centred at the foot of the window, with the text centred.
- Settings, Personal: the Keys group is drawn without a box round it, like the other groups.
- On a use case, the line above the title (Use cases, then the use case) is now in tracked capitals, and its link is underlined only when you point at it.
- The change card in the ask clark panel has a quieter look: a thin blue edge at the left, a light background, and a small flat Apply button at the left.
- The Milestones calendar's Week, Month and Quarter choices are one rounded group with the chosen one filled, and the Today button is as tall as the group.
- On a use case in Edit, Change status has a gap between its words and its arrow, and the buttons beside it are the same height. The Commitment menu now starts with the heading "Commitment".
- In the Milestones calendar's Quarter view, every milestone is a small marker with no text; its name, date and state are in its tooltip, and a click opens its peek.
- With Asking turned off, the ask clark panel keeps its greeting, its suggestion buttons (greyed out) and its box (greyed out), as well as the line that says asking is off.
- The "Answer" and "You" labels over the messages in the ask clark panel are no longer drawn. A screen reader still reads them.
- The clark logo in the top bar is now a button that goes Home (the Dashboard). A published file's logo is still not a button.
- The client Excel workbook's dates are left-aligned, so a date no longer runs into the Status that follows it.
- In the Word copy, the table columns are wide enough that an ID, a date or a status word is not broken across two lines. This changes the use cases, milestones, actions, risks and decisions tables.
- On Work items, the right-click menu on a row opens once at the pointer. The short lists for status, due date and owner open beside their entry; the menu itself no longer moves or changes size, and the Back entry is gone. In the short list, Left or Esc goes back to the entry.
- Milestones: while nothing is archived, the Show archived button and the empty Archived box are not drawn. The first archived milestone brings them back.

## What is fixed

- The profile circle in the top bar is now there when no project is open, with the entries that need no project, such as Settings.
- In Help, the last entry of the contents now lights when you scroll to the foot of the page.
- ask clark: the first time you open the panel, the words you have typed are kept and the box can be used while clark checks the connection; only Send waits. A good answer from the check is kept for five minutes, so a second open does not wait again.
- In the Client report menu, opening it with the mouse no longer draws a heavy ring on its first item. Opened by the keys, it still shows where you are.

## Known limits

- Updating from inside clark is still not switched on. To move from 1.0.2 to 1.0.3, install by hand: rename the old clark folder first, then unzip the download into a fresh empty folder. A plain unzip over the old folder merges old and new files.
- Editing a demo project keeps it: Remove demo data takes away only the demo projects you have not touched.
- In the client workbook only the ID, Priority, Original and Shown state are locked; the text cells stay editable, by design.
- Sorting is blocked on a protected sheet.
- The Undo on the messages for the Settings switches (Asking, Single-key shortcuts) sets the switch back, but it is not a line in History.

## Updating

- Updating from inside clark is not switched on yet: install a new version by downloading it and replacing the old one.
