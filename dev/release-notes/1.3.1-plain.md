# clark 1.3.1, Mac

This is the second build of the 1.3 line. It follows 1.3.0.

## What is new

### Theme

- Settings has a Theme card with two choices: Classic and Cards. Cards is the look clark has had until now. Classic is the same pages in the folder look, and it is the choice for a new install. The choice applies the next time the page loads.
- The page and the published file read the Theme. A report published before keeps its look, and so does an install that was already in use: both stay on Cards.

### Work items list

- The Id is its own column, in the link colour, after Priority.
- Priority shows as a pill: Urgent in red, High in amber, the rest in grey. An item with no priority keeps the muted dash.
- The list shows 50 rows. When there are more, a "Show all items" link opens the rest, and it then reads "Show only 50". The same choice is in View.
- The column menu offers Labels, off until you turn it on. The cell shows the item's labels, separated by commas.

### Peeks

- Every peek, Help included, now sits flush to the right edge, under the top bar and down to the bottom of the window. It slides in from the right over half a second. With reduced motion switched on it fades in over a fraction of a second. Below 1000 px wide a peek takes the whole width.

### Rich text

- The format bubble that appears over a selection has a last button, Draft with AI. It opens the same AI menu as a right-click. It shows only when AI is on, the field is not a single line and something is selected.

### Feedback

- Help and Settings pages have a Feedback tag on the right edge. It opens a small dialog: Problem, Idea or Question. The version of clark, the macOS version and the page you were on are added and shown to you. Nothing from your reports is added.

### Timeline

- The milestone timeline is resized with a grip centred under the strip, which you drag or move with the arrow keys. Home resets it. It is hidden in print.

## What is changed

- The top bar now follows a window resize at once, so a long project name no longer leaves the bar one size behind.
- Leaving a rich text field to the title of a peek now saves on a Mac in every focus order.
