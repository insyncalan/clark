# clark 1.3.1, Mac

This is the second build of the 1.3 line. It follows 1.3.0.

## What is new

### Feedback

- A Feedback tag sits on the right edge in Help and in Settings (not on the Dashboard, the lists, the peeks or the published page, and not on the Settings Feedback page). It opens a small dialog that slides in. You pick Problem, Idea or Question, write your note, and either copy it as text or open it in your mail app. The dialog adds the clark version, the macOS version and the page you were on; nothing from your reports is added.

### Work items list

- The column menu of the Work items list has a Labels column you can switch on and off.

### Report editor

- Leaving the rich editor by clicking elsewhere on the page now saves what you typed even when the browser gives no target for the click.

## What is changed

- The Windows download is the same zip, run in Docker, as in 1.3.0.
