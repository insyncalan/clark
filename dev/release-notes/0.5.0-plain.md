# clark 0.5.0 (Beta), Mac

Beta means the whole of clark is Beta until version 1.0.

Theme: change many work items at once, find use cases quickly, and a published page your client can filter.

## What is new

- With two or more work items selected in Edit, a bar above the table or board changes them together: close, reopen, status, owner, due date, priority, and the like. It is one step with one Undo.
- Items that cannot take a bulk change are left out and named, and clark asks first when more than 25 items would change.
- Right-click a work item, a card or a selection (or press Shift+F10 or the Menu key) to open the same list of changes.
- In Edit, the keys O, S, P, T and C change the owner, status, priority or due date, or close the item or the selection. They work only when the table, the board or the peek has the keyboard, and only if the single-key switch is on.
- When you change one cell of a row that is part of a selection, clark asks first and then changes that field on every selected row.
- Edit has a Use cases tab beside Milestones: a list with search, a status filter, a switch for On or Not on the dashboard, and sorting by reference, status or open work items.
- Click a use case in that list to open it on its own page, with Back to return to the list.
- On the published page, the Work items tab has a search box and the same filters as the editor (without the Client and Freshness filters), and the page remembers your choices in your own browser.
- A published page has a Use cases tab, for reading only. Each use case you have put on the dashboard is a row (reference, title, status) that opens in place to show its headline, its standing text, its open next steps and the milestones they point to. The tab is missing when no use case is shared.
- Publishing shows a spinner while it works and then a tick beside the success words.
- If an answer from ask clark would repeat clark's own hidden instructions, the answer is dropped and you see a plain refusal.
- The check for passwords and keys now also catches ones written in other languages, hidden in encoded text or put in a file's name. A question or a selection that holds one is refused, and a file with one in its name is left out of what ask clark reads.
- Help describes the Grey headers section style in the same words as Settings.
- The peek shows when an item was raised and last reviewed, and flags one not reviewed in 14 days.
- Word, Excel and Draft email now use the same colours and fonts as the page.
- In View, the Current state, Summary, This week and Next week sections now show their saved text, or the quiet line "Nothing recorded." when there is none, instead of "Not shown on this page yet." (Edit and the published file are unchanged.)
- The At a glance tiles now share the row equally, with Project health twice as wide as the others, and four tiles still sit on one line at 900 px. In Edit, a long tile label wraps instead of running under the move button and the eye.
- At 900 px wide or less, the short lines at the right edge of the page (the contents rail) become an "On this page" button at the bottom right; press it for the list of sections. This holds in the editor and on a published page.

## Not yet

- Signing in to Claude or OpenAI from inside clark is not in this version.
- Updating clark from Settings.
- Draft email to the owner from the right-click menu. (The right-click menu above holds the bulk changes only.)
- Archive and Restore for work items and milestones.
- Arrow keys on work items while the peek is open.
- Adding or editing a progress tracker.
