# clark 1.0.6 (Beta), Mac

This is the 1.0 build. It is still marked Beta: it works and is tested, but expect rough edges and changes between versions.

## What is new

### Assign to

- The Assign to picker opens on Recent and People. Typing searches the people register (name, alias, role, supplier) and shows eight at most; "And N more. Keep typing to find them." says when there are more. With no register, the project's own names are used.
- If the name you type is not on the list, the picker offers "Add <name> as a new person" and "Keep the name only". The first takes a name and an optional email, saves the person and assigns them in one step, and one Undo takes both back. A name already on the list offers "Use this one".
- Help now explains how Assign to works.

### Settings

- Brands: a new Settings page. "Add a brand from a file you were given, and clark uses its colours, fonts and pictures. Nothing changes until you choose Install." You can Install, Replace or Remove a brand, and Undo it. "Back to the default look" returns to the standard look.
- Local folder: Publishing in Settings has a Local folder box with Choose folder, saved once. "Always saved. Publish's save window opens in this folder; with none chosen, it opens where you last saved this report." Clear takes it away again.
- Profile is the first card in Settings. It holds Your name, Alias and Your email, which were under Personal. Every message that points to them now says "Settings › Profile".

### Work items

- Archive is on the milestone peek too, with the same toast and Undo as a work item.
- The Details tab of the peek shows a Source line. The Activity tab says where an update came from: "Updated from <file>, <date>, by <name>", and "By Word import (<file>)". An item made by ask clark has the source "Added by ask clark".
- In the filter box, Backspace or Delete beside a half-typed word such as status:Open removes the whole word. The list of fields opens when you press Space, not when you click in, and each field in it has an icon.
- One editor is used everywhere you write: Latest update, a milestone's Description, the Link card, One document and Callout words, Text and image words, the Movement internal note and the At a glance Reason. It has the same / menu in each place.
- Filters you accept in the filter box now show as chips, each with its own remove button. Removing one takes away only that filter.
- The list of values for a filter field now shows how many items each value leaves. If you type a field that does not exist, the box lists the fields that do.

### Word and PDF graphics

- A Word report carries the milestone plan, the Health bar and the progress bars as pictures. The labels stay real Word text. A Word report with a Gantt tab gets a Gantt section: the lines as pictures on one time line, with "{n} of {m} lines shown". Opening a Word report that clark made no longer counts clark's own pictures as "pictures, drawings or text boxes were not read".

## What is changed

### Draft email

- The Draft email button is called "Open in your mail app". The Draft email box fits inside the window.
- A client wording card with no wording of its own publishes the item's title.

### Settings

- Your own brand folder is now `branding`, beside `.app`. The first time 1.0.6 starts it copies a folder called `system/branding` from an older clark into `branding`, if `branding` has nothing in it yet; the old folder is left as it is. If `system/branding` is a link, clark does not copy it and says so in its log: copy the files into `branding` by hand.
- The Claude tile says "Through your Claude subscription, on this computer."
- A done people paste says "N people added or updated".
- At 900 px and wider the links to the Settings cards are a list down the left.
- At phone width the People table and the edit rows fit the window, and the Settings chips and profile menu rows are 44 px high.
- Settings is no longer a tab in the top bar. Open it from the profile circle's menu, by #settings in the address, or from Published reports.
- On a phone, the Use cases list, the project buttons in Settings and the Edit use case row fit the window with no sideways scroll.

### Work items

- The star ("Change an item with words") is gone from the peek bar and from the use case page. Two slashes still open the words box in both places.
- Help says: "A number is never used again, and **Undo** does not take it back."
- The top of the peek is tighter, with less empty space before Latest update.
- Owner, Due, Priority and Status in the Actions table read as plain words; the box shows when you point at it or tab to it.
- On a use case, the gap between "Link an existing work item" and "See all" is even.
- The examples row under the filter line no longer makes the area taller.
- The right-click menu has an icon on each entry.
- Pickers and menus fade out when they close, and stay still when your computer asks for less motion.
- On a use case, the eye and its words are now one button: "On the dashboard" or "Not on the dashboard".
- The page remembers where you were on each screen: going to another page and coming back by tab or by Back keeps your place.
- A changed section that you drag over keeps its glow and shows where it will drop.
- Switching between the Work items views keeps the view you chose. A view you have changed shows a dot on its tab, and Save view and Discard changes act on that tab.
- A row you click in a Work items list no longer shows a check mark. The row keeps its height, and its border, left bar and tint still show it is selected.
- In Edit, the Quote and Link preview no longer have a blue bar down the left side. A Use cases row you point at is tinted grey, and a Help note has a thin grey frame.
- The words of the right-click menu entries are no longer bold.
- In the right-click menu, a list (status, due, owner) opens only when the pointer rests on its entry, so moving across the entries no longer flashes lists open.
- When your computer asks for less motion, the Earlier, Later and Today buttons on the milestone timeline jump instead of scrolling smoothly, and the contents card, the Legend pop-up and the tab underlines stop animating.
- In dark, buttons, icons and the keyboard focus ring are easier to see.
- Next steps in a Use cases row show only the number of the linked work item.
- In the item rows and the RAID cards, ids, dates and owners never wrap; only the title is cut, with an ellipsis.

### Gantt

- The Gantt header row stays in view when there are many lines.
- A long label is shortened with "…" instead of running into the next one, and lines with no dates are counted.
- Headings and the Today flag are no longer cut off at 900 px wide.
- Turning reduced motion off no longer makes every bar grow again.
- Print gives the Gantt as it is shown on the screen.

### Publishing

- If words from a row you left out of the report, from a hidden section, from a left-out document title or from a left-out Gantt line appear inside another row that stays in, publishing stops and says "Nothing was published. The report has something in it that must not go to the client", followed by what was found. The Word copy for the client stops the same way. A left-out text that is exactly the whole of another value still publishes. Publishing also stops when left-out words are split by spaces, tabs or non-breaking spaces. The Word report also stops, as the web publish already does, when a file saved in the Local folder is inside the client folder, so a copy meant to stay local cannot end up in the client's folder.
- The PDF print tab shows the very file that was just published, not a second copy built afterwards.

### Help

- Headings in Help now step down in size: the page title is the largest, then the opening heading, then each section heading. The Start here heading follows the same step.
- Help no longer names keys the page does not have.

### Reports

- With separate section cards, the report's top band has rounded corners and the card shadow, so it stands alone like the cards under it.
- When a brand has a cover picture, the report's top band fills with it, faded in from the left edge and clear on the right.

## What is fixed

### Draft email

- A bad sent date now says: "The day the email was sent must be a real date. Reload the page and try again. Nothing was saved."
- "I sent it" for an item kept out of emails says: "One of the ticked items can't be recorded: it is hidden, closed or labelled to be kept out of emails. Nothing was saved. Reload the page and tick again."
- When you press I sent it, the history now says who was emailed, when, and what the email was about.
- The Draft email says the same words for an archived project wherever it shows that sentence.
- When "I sent it" gets no answer, the line says the press may or may not be saved to the history, and to look in the history before you press I sent it again.

### Settings

- An organisation or role longer than 100 characters is not accepted: "the organisation is too long (at most 100 characters). Shorten it and try again. Nothing was changed."

### Work items

- An empty Workstream in the archived peek says "Not set".
- Clearing the Week ending box says: "Week ending cannot be left empty. The old week ending date was kept."
- The calendar's "No milestones in this period" line shows at laptop widths.
- Resetting a column width from the keyboard says "Column width reset", as the mouse does.
- When a Hold pushes the oldest lines out of the note, it says so: "Hold recorded. The oldest lines of the note were removed to fit. Undo."
- A closed risk or issue that was never rated now reads "Closed · Not rated" on one line, also on a phone-width window.
- Pressing Draft in Latest update keeps your cursor in the box, and what you type next goes into the drafted text. One press makes one draft and one Undo.
- In the box for a project's progress words, pressing Escape puts the old words back, also when you click into the box straight away.
- The Legend help button is no longer printed on the Milestones timeline.
- An undone step that belongs to another project reads "This change can't be undone from here" on the Activity tab.

### Safety fixes

- A line such as token: or api_key: followed by a quoted word is now left out of a folder read, like a password.
- The note that part of your report looks like a password or a key says the same thing everywhere: "Part of your report looks like a password or a key, so it was left out and the answer may be missing something. Take it out of the item if you want it included."
- The Docker starter checks the data folder is not in OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive or Google Drive, then starts.
- The publish check on the PDF now lets through only the footer's own words (Publication, Published, Page); every other word must be in the report.
- If a publish fails after a link was replaced, the link is put back.
- If an update could not move your data to the new clark, the put-back now still runs, and a new server that gets stuck is cleaned up.
- A key that starts with sk-, pk- or rk-, in any letter case, is now left out of a folder read, like other keys.
- A stored list or dict in a priority or status no longer breaks the Word report; it shows Not rated.
- A long random-looking run of 25 to 31 letters and digits is now left out of a folder read, like a longer one. Ordinary names joined by _ or - (such as Project_Tulip_Phase2_Rev03_v2) are still read.

### Dashboard

- Hiding a section that fills the screen on the Dashboard, then switching from Edit to View, no longer makes the page jump.

## Known limits

- Editing a demo project keeps it: Remove demo data takes away only the demo projects you have not touched.
- A Gantt sheet with more than 500 lines stops the import; split it into smaller sheets.
- The client workbook is a copy for the client. It cannot be imported back.
- Sorting is blocked on a protected sheet.
