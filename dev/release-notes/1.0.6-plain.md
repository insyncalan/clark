# clark 1.0.6 (Beta), Mac

This is the 1.0 build. It is still marked Beta: it works and is tested, but expect rough edges and changes between versions.

## What is new

### Assign to

- The Assign to picker opens on Recent and People. Typing searches the people register (name, alias, role, supplier) and shows eight at most; "And N more. Keep typing to find them." says when there are more. With no register, the project's own names are used.

### Settings

- Brands: a new Settings page. "Add a brand from a file you were given, and clark uses its colours, fonts and pictures. Nothing changes until you choose Install." You can Install, Replace or Remove a brand, and Undo it. "Back to the default look" returns to the standard look.
- Local folder: Publishing in Settings has a Local folder box with Choose folder, saved once. "Always saved. Publish's save window opens in this folder; with none chosen, it opens where you last saved this report." Clear takes it away again.

### Work items

- Archive is on the milestone peek too, with the same toast and Undo as a work item.
- The Details tab of the peek shows a Source line.
- The Activity tab says where an update came from: "Updated from <file>, <date>, by <name>", and "By Word import (<file>)".
- An item made by ask clark has the source "Added by ask clark".

### Word and PDF graphics

- A Word report carries the milestone plan, the Health bar and the progress bars as pictures. The labels stay real Word text.
- A Word report with a Gantt tab gets a Gantt section: the lines as pictures on one time line, with "{n} of {m} lines shown".

## What is changed

### Draft email

- The Draft email button is called "Open in your mail app".
- The Draft email box fits inside the window.
- A client wording card with no wording of its own publishes the item's title.

### Settings

- The Claude tile says "Through your Claude subscription, on this computer."
- A done people paste says "N people added or updated".

### Work items

- The star ("Change an item with words") is gone from the peek bar and from the use case page. Two slashes still open the words box in both places.
- Help says: "A number is never used again, and **Undo** does not take it back."
- The top of the peek is tighter, with less empty space before Latest update.
- Owner, Due, Priority and Status in the Actions table read as plain words; the box shows when you point at it or tab to it.
- On a use case, the gap between "Link an existing work item" and "See all" is even.
- The examples row under the filter line no longer makes the area taller.
- The right-click menu has an icon on each entry.
- Pickers and menus fade out when they close, and stay still when your computer asks for less motion.

### Word and PDF graphics

- Opening a Word report that clark made no longer counts clark's own pictures as "pictures, drawings or text boxes were not read".

### Publishing

- If words from a row you left out of the report, or from a hidden section, appear inside another row that stays in, publishing now stops and says so, so those words do not reach the client.

## What is fixed

### Draft email

- A bad sent date now says: "The day the email was sent must be a real date. Reload the page and try again. Nothing was saved."
- "I sent it" for an item kept out of emails says: "One of the ticked items can't be recorded: it is hidden, closed or labelled to be kept out of emails. Nothing was saved. Reload the page and tick again."

### Settings

- An organisation or role longer than 100 characters is not accepted: "the organisation is too long (at most 100 characters). Shorten it and try again. Nothing was changed."

### Work items

- An empty Workstream in the archived peek says "Not set".
- Clearing the Week ending box says: "Week ending cannot be left empty. The old week ending date was kept."
- The calendar's "No milestones in this period" line shows at laptop widths.
- Resetting a column width from the keyboard says "Column width reset", as the mouse does.
- On a Mac, a source link in an answer opens the saved PDF in Chrome.
- When a Hold pushes the oldest lines out of the note, it says so: "Hold recorded. The oldest lines of the note were removed to fit. Undo."

### Safety fixes

- A line such as token: or api_key: followed by a quoted word is now left out of a folder read, like a password.
- The note that part of your report looks like a password or a key says the same thing everywhere: "Part of your report looks like a password or a key, so it was left out and the answer may be missing something. Take it out of the item if you want it included."
- The Docker starter checks the data folder is not in OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive or Google Drive, then starts.
- The publish check on the PDF now lets through only the footer's own words (Publication, Published, Page); every other word must be in the report.
- If a publish fails after a link was replaced, the link is put back.

## Known limits

- Editing a demo project keeps it: Remove demo data takes away only the demo projects you have not touched.
- A Gantt sheet with more than 500 lines stops the import; split it into smaller sheets.
- The client workbook is a copy for the client. It cannot be imported back.
- Sorting is blocked on a protected sheet.
