# clark 0.4.0 (Beta), Mac

Beta means the whole of clark is Beta until version 1.0.

Theme: arrange your dashboard your way, see milestones and use cases at a glance, and trust the Excel export and the safety net.

## What is new

- In Edit, drag a section to move it. Drop it between rows for a full-width section, or on the left or right half of another section to sit side by side.
- Each section has one quiet control in the left margin. It holds move, place beside, add below, leave out of the published page and delete.
- The Add a section box offers ready-made templates, such as Text and Needs from you. Pictures, figures, files and risk cards are listed and come in a later release.
- The Milestones page shows a Calendar beside the Timeline. The switch shows an icon and a word.
- A Use cases section on the Dashboard lists every use case of the project with its detail.
- Every change to a work item, milestone or use case is kept as history: when, who, which field, and from what to what. The peek shows it in its Activity tab, and the Excel export has a History sheet.
- The Excel export has a Cover sheet. It shows the current state, At a glance, the text sections, and who exported the file and when, with a caution that later changes are not included.
- The Excel export uses the brand font on its data sheets and freezes the header rows.
- An Excel file that is edited and saved again still imports against its original export.
- Settings has a Safety page. It lists your backups and lets you preview and restore one.
- Settings has a Logs and diagnostics page. Choose Normal or Debug, and use Copy diagnostics to share a report. No client content is logged.
- A brand's cover picture now shows on the page instead of a missing image.
- Press V on the Dashboard for View and E for Edit.
- One Esc now closes both date panels when you edit a date in a row.
- In Edit, the Priority, Status and Due cells on Work items no longer wrap or look cramped.
- A bad value for the idle timer is ignored, so clark still closes itself when it should.
- Help says that PDF files are not read yet when ask clark reads your folders.
- In Personal, the four dashboard section styles are shown as picture cards, and the published page uses the style you chose.
- The peek shows coloured icons for status, priority and date.

## Not yet

- Update clark from Settings. The checks on a downloaded update are in, but you cannot install one from Settings yet.
- Right-click menu on work items, with Draft email to the owner.
- Archive and Restore for work items and milestones.
