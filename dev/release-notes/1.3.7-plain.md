# clark 1.3.7, Mac

This is a polish release on 1.3. It is a full release.

## What is new

### Classic

- Milestones: the Timeline / Calendar switch and the Week / Month / Quarter switch have a thin grey frame, a thin grey line between the halves, buttons 28 px high with padding 3px 14px, and grey text on the half that is not chosen. [1315, 1355]
- The project name in the bar of the editor stays on one line on every page; a long name is cut with "…". [1341]
- At a glance (Edit): each built-in tile except Project health has a bin that deletes it, with an Undo and the message "Deleted:" and the tile name. The hint reads "Drag a tile to move it, or hold Alt and press an arrow key. The eye hides a tile from the published page; the bin deletes it. Add a tile brings it back." The tiles are 12 px apart. [1336]
- At a glance: the Project health reason sits under the pill, and the status dot in View is 8 by 8 px. [1336]

### Text and Image Side by Side

- "Text and image" is now called "Text and Image Side by Side" in the template picker and in Help. A new section starts as version 2 of this template. [1232]
- Edit has a toolbar, "Image and layout tools": Shape (Fill the height of the text, Wide 16:9, 4:3, square, whole image), where the image sits (left or right; top or underneath at half width), the width of the image (a third, a half, two thirds; full width only), Swap image and text, Crop, Reset framing, Replace and Remove. A focus dot (28 px) sets what stays in view. [1232]
- An optional Heading, a Text box, a Caption and "Describe the image" (for people who cannot see it). An image is made smaller to 3,000 px wide at most for print, and the note under the toolbar says so. [1232]
- In View the optional Heading is drawn above the picture and the words, and a half-width section keeps its "image underneath" choice. [1232]
- The Word report now writes this section: the section heading, the optional heading, the image (a small one is centred at its own size) with its caption, and the words. The image comes first when it is on the left. [1232]

### Ask clark

- Stop works at every stage. A Stop before the answer starts sends nothing, a Stop while clark is reading your data sends nothing, and an answer that still arrives after Stop is not shown: the line "Stopped. Nothing was changed." is shown instead. [1293]
- When a passage that is sent holds hidden characters, clark says so in a plain note, with the count. [1293]
- Ask clark reads the project's Current state even when that section is hidden in the report. [1367]
- Ask clark reads a project's workstreams (name, status and the date it was created). The lead is never read. [1367]
- A file added to a question keeps its room (its passages and its characters) before the project and the folders take theirs. [1367]
- Settings, Files: a "Files" switch for all projects (text, Markdown and CSV, 5 files of 10 MB, for that question only), and in a project's own tab a switch to turn files off for that project only. When files are off the paperclip and the drop area are gone from the ask panel. The paperclip says "Add a file" and the picker offers text, Markdown and CSV files only. [1294]
- Settings, project tab: the order is now Slash commands, Files, Chats, then Usage. [1294]
- Settings, source folders: while a folder is read the line says "Indexing… N%" (it was "Reading N of M files."). A finished folder says "100% indexed", with how many files could not be read and a "(why)" link that opens the list. A folder with no readable file says "No files to index". A screen reader hears the progress at 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 percent only. [1371]

## Behind the scenes (no change you see)
