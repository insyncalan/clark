# clark 1.3.12, Mac

This is a polish release on 1.3. It is a full release for the rows listed below. The macOS test shards were not all run for this cut (shard 3 of 6 failed in the hand run), so the Mac is covered by the Mac smoke set and the rows' own tests.

## What is new

### Classic

- Work items: the filter chip is a rounded rectangle (6 px corners, at least 25 px high) at 1400 and 900. [1290]
- Work items: "Group by" is also offered in the filter field, with its own chip. The Group by icon and menu stay where they are. [1304]
- The Add menu icon is grey, its words start 36 px in, and the menu is 224 px wide. [1462]
- The Dashboard peek shows the Latest update box. [1470]
- Milestones: the grip under the timeline shows when the milestones overflow, not when there are three or more. [1570]
- The peek: the label "Shown to the client" is 12 px, muted, with a cyan eye, and the "Show history" link is 13 px in the mock-up blue. [1576]
- Image section: the small-image width is 600 px. [1580]
- A section with Tile off is clear: the banded section is transparent, and a half-width left section draws no strip beside it. [1579, 1606]
- Documents and links: several files can be added at once, in the file window and by dropping them. [1593]
- Work items footer: the "Review all" buttons keep to one line. [1615]
- Report headline, Edit at 900 px, and the owner column of the Work items list at 900 px: measured and pinned. [1428, 1443]

### Client wording

- The work item field "Client wording" is now called "Client Message" wherever a person reads it, and the "Client visible" tag and eye are hidden in published reports. [1466]

## Behind the scenes (no change you see)

- Pinned with tests only: Milestones, the Work items footer, the audit workflow (report only, no push trigger), and the Classic peek. [1314, 1577]
