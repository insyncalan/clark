# clark 1.0.2 (Beta), Mac

This is the 1.0 build. It is still marked Beta because updating from inside clark is not switched on yet.

This version does not change how clark stores your data. Your project files open as they did in 1.0.1.

Theme: a quieter use case page, ask clark that can change a use case, a clearer Excel cover, and a safer start.

## What is new

- ask clark can now suggest a change to a use case: its Headline, its Where it stands, or its status. The change comes as a card with the words before and after, as for a work item. Nothing is saved until you press Apply, and Undo takes it back in one step. ask clark can read a use case's Commitment flag but never changes it, and it cannot add, rename, hide or archive a use case.
- A start that finds a folder of mixed versions now stops with a plain message instead of starting the wrong one. If you unzip a newer download over an older clark folder, the files can be part old and part new; clark says "These clark files are not all from the same version, so clark will not start. Move the clark folder aside (rename it) and unzip the download again." and does not start.
- /health, the page clark answers to say it is running, now also says which version is running.

## What is changed

- On the Use cases tab and on a use case, the choice to show a use case on the report is the eye alone. The words are the tooltip: "On the dashboard" or "Not on the dashboard". The Home dashboard column heading, the words beside the eye and the Take off the dashboard and Put on the dashboard button are gone. In Edit, click the eye to switch; Undo puts it back.
- Settings: above 900 px wide, the links to its cards (Publishing, Personal and the rest) are a list down the left, with the cards at the right. The list stays under the top bar as you scroll, and the link of the card you are reading is marked. At 900 px wide and narrower the links are the row of chips under the title, as before. Choosing a link still goes to its card.
- The Excel cover has a larger title, with a thick blue edge under the dark band. The line under the title gives the week ending as a day name, day, month in words and year, for example "Week ending Friday 6 November 2026".
- The Excel cover takes its logo and its cover picture only from your branding folder. With no branding folder, the logo is clark's own icon and there is no cover picture. With a branding folder that has no logo, the cover has no logo, and clark's mark is never put on it. A cover picture, when the brand has one, sits in a dark row of its own under the title. A file that leads out of the branding folder is never read.
- When several workbooks are held for importing from Excel at once, a workbook that has not been checked yet is let go before one you have checked and are about to put in.
- START-HERE.txt now says "Double-click clark.command to start clark."
- Importing from Excel: a sheet with more than 2,000 rows is listed as "more than 2,000 rows" in the box that lists the sheets.
- The first line of START-HERE.txt in a public download no longer names the repository. It reads "This download holds none of the team's private notes."
- When you start clark with a data folder of your own (the start option --data, or Docker), the daily log is kept inside that folder, in its logs folder, and Settings shows that place. Without it the log stays in the system's Logs folder for clark.
- Help says what the slash key does inside Help: it goes to the search box, unless the letter keys are turned off in Settings, Personal, Keys; and the ask key leaves Help alone: "except while Help is open".
- A brand's own circle picture for the top bar (picture data inside its styling file, in the .logo rule) no longer stops Preview in a new tab or Publish. A comment in that file can no longer hide an outside address or a picture from the publish check. When the styling file does stop a report, the message says which of two things it was. For an outside address: "The brand's styling file has a link to an address outside the report, so the report can't go out. Ask whoever looks after the brand to remove it." For a picture in another form: "The brand's styling file has a picture in a form clark cannot put in the report, so the report can't go out. Ask whoever looks after the brand to change it."
- When a live document is left out of the published file because its link, title or kind cannot be used, the log now says so, naming the document by its ID only.
- When a published report cannot be put back in its folder under its name, the message now reads "The report could not be put back in the folder under its name. It is still in the folder under a hidden name."

## What is fixed

- Pressing E on the Dashboard no longer moves the section you are reading. Switching between View and Edit keeps the section you are looking at where it was, even when only a thin strip of the section above shows at the top.
- The box that asks "Remove demo data?" (and every other box that opens over the page) now opens below the top bar instead of over it.
- bash clark.command --help, and a mistaken option, now show the command you typed in the usage line: "usage: bash clark.command".
- clark.command no longer leaves a __pycache__ folder in the clark folder.
- The meet clark folder's two guide PDFs and the overview picture were regenerated; the overview picture now shows bash clark.command, the same as the README and START-HERE.txt.

## Behind the scenes

- The top bar's hover and border colours use the colour tokens; the look is the same.
- The Docker compose file, the Docker notes and the shown-path comment in the program say where the data is mounted.
- The Releasing steps carry the changes made after 1.0.1: the freeze, tagging the gate commit, macOS in two shards, signing and the meet clark folder.
- New tests cover a failed upgrade of your data when clark starts (your old data stays usable, and an older clark refuses data from a newer one), unzipping a newer version over an older folder, the top bar tabs at a long project name, and that Help never says the Excel import takes CSV.

## Known limits

- Updating from inside clark is still not switched on. To move from 1.0.1 to 1.0.2, install by hand: rename the old clark folder first, then unzip the download into a fresh empty folder. A plain unzip over the old folder merges old and new files.
- Help still describes the links to the Settings cards as a row of chips under the title. In a window wider than 900 px they are a list down the left; the card names and order are the same.
- On Work items, a row opens in the peek on a double-click; a single click selects it.
- Editing a demo project keeps it: Remove demo data takes away only the demo projects you have not touched.

## Updating

- Updating from inside clark is not switched on yet: install a new version by downloading it and replacing the old one.
