# clark 1.3.13, Mac

This is a polish release on 1.3. It is a full release for the rows listed below. macOS: see the cut record.

## What is new

### Classic

- Menus: the icons in the Add, Group by and Move to menus are plain, with no colour tone. [1540]
- Text and Image Side by Side: when you add it, Edit shows a sample picture above "Choose an image", and the prompts "Write here." and "Say what the picture shows." The sample goes when you choose your own picture; nothing of it is saved or shown in View or the published report. [1251]

## Known limits

- Pages you add and mark "Shown to the client" do not appear yet in the published report: it has no tab for them, though their content is saved inside the file.
- The client's Excel copy has no Documents sheet for the Documents section that new reports start with; reports that use the older Supporting Documents section still get one (#1683).

## Behind the scenes (no change you see)

- Pinned with tests only: the Columns menu fits a short window, and the View menu stays inside the window at 1400. [1540]
