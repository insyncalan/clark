# Hand-check sheet for clark 1.3.3

Written for the person who looks at the screens after the release. One line for each thing a person can see that changed; only what a test cannot decide. Hand checks never hold up a release.

**Sample data only.** The fictional demo projects. Run `bash clark.command --demo` in the unzipped clark folder. Laptop only: Chrome at 1400 px wide. Do not press Export, Save or Publish on real data.

Each row: what to check by hand, and what you should see. Tick each line; write what you saw where it fails.

- [ ] Settings has a grey menu panel on the left, with the page's thin rule beside the whole page. The Projects card has a filled Add project button and bold column headers.
- [ ] The Milestones page is flat (no frame); its timeline and Progress bars follow the design, and the Reset link is gone.
- [ ] On Work items, the chosen layout tile is grey, and the "Show all" link works on the page you publish. Configure now offers Sort by Due date, Priority, Type and Owner.
- [ ] On Work items in Edit, the gear is 32 px and no longer touches the Changed tag; Owner stays on one line.
- [ ] Use cases is a flat table with a column chooser. A grouped list shows each group's name as a heading, with the column names once at the top. The filter is a boxed input with a magnifier.
- [ ] Main buttons are navy with white text and 8 px corners, no longer aqua pills.
- [ ] Every menu has the same look: 15 px lines, 6 px corners, an icon 16 px beside its words. A menu taller than the window scrolls, or opens above its button.
- [ ] The filter line shows a typed word in a small grey box that is not clipped at the start of the line. The peek fades in, and the Ask clark panel slides in from the right.
- [ ] Settings > Theme shows Classic only. A copy that had Cards now shows Classic.
- [ ] The quick bar (Cmd+K) shows "Cmd+Enter" on a Mac, or "Ctrl+Enter" elsewhere, on the Ask row, to send the question.
- [ ] In Settings, the ask clark cards and their tabs follow one order, so Tab moves through them the way the page reads.
- [ ] The suggestion pills follow the page you are on and the items named in the answer.
- [ ] Open in your mail app now goes through clark itself, so it opens your mail app from the Feedback tag and from Help.
- [ ] The profile menu now reads Preview, Export to Excel, Publish, and the tooltip follows that order.
- [ ] On the Dashboard, a mouse wheel over the Work items box at its end carries on scrolling the page (Classic).
- [ ] At a glance in Edit: each tile has more room inside and shows its status dot before the label.
- [ ] The dialog's heading is 17 px with a little more room around it, as in the design.
- [ ] Pressing Copy or Open in your mail app with nothing written shows a red line under the box: "Please write a line first."
