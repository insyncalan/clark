# Hand-check sheet for clark 1.1.1

Written for the person who looks at the screens after the release. One line for each thing a person can see that changed; only what a test cannot decide. Hand checks never hold up a release.

**Sample data only.** The fictional demo projects. Run `bash clark.command --demo` in the unzipped clark folder. Laptop only: Chrome at 1400 px wide, then again at 900 px. Light and dark where a line says so. Do not press Export, Save or Publish on real data.

Each row: id, what to check by hand, and what you should see. Tick each line; write what you saw where it fails.

Screens changed since 1.1.0: peeks, Configure, empty states, Dashboard Edit drag.

## Peeks (rows 1256, 1258, 1182, 1183)

- [ ] 1256: In Edit open an action that is overdue, then a risk and an issue. Look at the row of chips under the title, at 1400 and 900. You should see the chips side by side as one flat row with no gaps between them. The overdue action's Status chip says Open and its Due chip shows the date in red with a small red "overdue N days" badge. The chip itself keeps its normal colour.
- [ ] 1256: In Edit open a risk and an issue and look at the Details. You should see Workstream, Milestone, Raised and Use case, then the Client visible line, laid out like an action's Details. The Status chip says Open (Closed or Cancelled once it is).
- [ ] 1256: In View, and in a published file, open a risk. You should see a read-only Client visible or Client not visible line in its Details. It does not change when you click it.
- [ ] 1258: Open a peek in Edit and in View, at 1400 and 900. You should see the title and the tabs (Details, Activity) in the same sizes as the rest of the page, the footer keys at a readable size, and the history tile fully in view after it slides in.
- [ ] 1182: Open an open risk that has a rating. Look at its Rating chip. You should see the colour word (Red, Amber or Green) with the level (Urgent, High, Medium or Low) as a small badge beside it. An open risk with no rating shows no badge.
- [ ] 1183: Open an issue, click the Rating chip, and pick a cell on the 3 x 3 grid. Then look at its Status list. You should see one click set both impact and likelihood. The Status list holds no Red, Amber or Green. A closed issue says "<colour> kept".
- [ ] 1184: Open a milestone in Edit. Click each chip: State, Owner, Progress, Forecast date. You should see State offer Forecast, On track, At risk, Slipped and Achieved, Progress show ten blocks you press (pressing the block that matches the progress sets it back to 0), and the Forecast date chip open a date picker. Esc closes each one with the focus back on the chip. A late forecast shows "overdue N days" under the date.
- [ ] 1184: In the same milestone peek, look below the chips. You should see a Latest update box, then Description, Agreed date, Workstream, Use case and Client wording, and the Client visible line at the end.
- [ ] 1256: Open the Activity tab of an item whose date changed. You should see its dates written DD-MM-YYYY, for example 05-11-2026, not 2026-11-05.

## Configure (row 1230)

- [ ] In Edit, on the Dashboard, find the Milestones section. You should see a Configure button in its header. Click it. The box is called Configure milestones and has "Milestones shown in the list" (a number or All), "Viewers can scroll the list", and a status tracker setting: Top, Bottom or Off. Each change saves at once and has one Undo.
- [ ] Set the number to 3 with scrolling On, then Off, at 1400 and 900. You should see only that many rows, and a note under the list saying how many show at a time and whether viewers can scroll.
- [ ] Look at the Dashboard's Milestones list. You should see a heading row: Milestone, State, Agreed, Expected or achieved, Progress. The status tracker sits where you set it. The scale choice at the top right is plain text tabs.
- [ ] Publish the demo to a test folder and open the file. You should see the same number of rows and the same tracker place as in Edit, and no Configure button.
- [ ] On the Milestones page in Edit, take a milestone off the page with its eye, then choose "Add a milestone from your project record". You should see a table with a search box ("Search by name, ID or state"), a filters button, a tick in the heading that selects every row shown, and Cancel and Add.

## Empty states (row 1234)

- [ ] In Edit open the Use case summary on a project whose use cases are all on the page already, and open the add box. You should see one sentence, "Everything is already on the page.", shown once under the title and not again in the body.
- [ ] In the Use case summary and on the Use cases page, look at the Next steps column. You should see the number of open steps as a round badge, and "No open steps" as plain text when there are none.
- [ ] In Edit look at the Use case summary header. You should see Configure only: no Add use case button.

## Dashboard Edit drag (row 1214)

- [ ] Drag a section by its move button over another section. You should see a line under the page that says what letting go would do (for example Release to put "A" beside "B", half width each), a ghost of the title, and a dashed outline where it came from.
- [ ] Press Esc during a drag, then let go. You should see nothing move.
- [ ] Focus a section's move button and press Alt+Right, then Alt+Left. You should see the section go beside the next one, then back to full width. A toast says what was done and offers Undo.
- [ ] On a touch screen (or a touch device in Chrome's tools), swipe quickly down the page over a move button, then hold a finger on it for half a second and drag. You should see the quick swipe scroll the page, and the held finger start the drag.
- [ ] Start a drag and press Alt+Tab to another window, then come back. You should see the drag has ended and nothing moved.
