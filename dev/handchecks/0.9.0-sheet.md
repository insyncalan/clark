# Hand-check sheet for clark 0.9.0 (DRAFT: written after 0.8.0 was published)

Written for the person who looks at the screens after the release. Only what a test cannot decide; everything else is covered by `tests/test_v090.py`. Hand checks never hold up a release.

**Sample data only.** The fictional demo projects. Run `python3 -m clark --demo --open` in the top folder of the release. Laptop only: Chrome at 1400 px wide, then again at 900 px. Light and dark where a line says so.

Each row: id, what to check by hand, what you should see, and how it is covered. Tick each line; write what you saw where it fails.

## For the Head at the cut

- **Carried from 0.8.0.** Everything that waited at the 0.8.0 cut is in the table above with its pull request. Merging one of those pull requests makes `tests/test_v090.py` fail its row (once the cut sets 0.9.0): move the row to COVERED with its covering test, add a line to the notes and a row above. Until the cut it skips.
- **Words the owner approved on 04-11-2026, to quote once they are on screen.** The not reviewed tile (#160, PR 523): 'Not reviewed for more than 14 days, of 8 open items'. The built tile in 0.8.0 reads "More than 14 days, of 8 open items" under the title "Not reviewed" (`glanceTiles.js` `staleTile`), so the approved line needs a small code change before the notes can quote it. The week picker (#344, PR 529) is built with the label "Open a published week", the first entry "Published weeks" and "Dashboard as at a day…" (`logic/weekPicker.js`); the notes may quote these once the owner says these are the words he approved.
- **Data layout.** 0.8.0 reads layout 15. `SCHEMA_AT_0_8_0` in `tests/test_v090.py` is 15: if a pull request for 0.9.0 adds a layout step, the notes must carry the layout paragraph (the notes test fails until they do).
- **Version.** The cut commit sets `__version__ = "0.9.0"`; until then the cut checks in `tests/test_v090.py` skip.
- **Not in the 0.9 cut yet.** Nothing is held back by name. #614 (PR 540, the ring pace) and #577 are approved but not merged.
