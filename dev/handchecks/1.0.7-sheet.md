# Hand-check sheet for clark 1.0.7

Written for the person who looks at the screens after the release. One line for each thing a person can see that changed; only what a test cannot decide. Hand checks never hold up a release.

**Sample data only.** The fictional demo projects. Run `bash clark.command --demo` in the unzipped clark folder. Laptop only: Chrome at 1400 px wide, then again at 900 px. Light and dark where a line says so. Do not press Export, Save or Publish on real data.

**24 px rule.** Wherever a row changes the size of a control, that control must be at least 24 by 24 px at 1400 and at 900. The row says so.

Each row: id, what to check by hand, and what you should see. Tick each line; write what you saw where it fails. The status table and the pull request numbers are added at the cut, in this sheet.

## Status of every row

Base: everything merged to main after the tag v1.0.6. The Id is the plan row; the PR is the clark repository's pull request. A row is MERGED, IN REVIEW (a pull request is open or being reviewed), WAITING (not started) or DROPPED. At the cut every row is MERGED or DROPPED. This first batch lists the rows already merged; the cut bookkeeping adds the rest.

## By eye on this release

| Id | Check | You should see |
|----|-------|----------------|
| S.1 | Settings: open it from the profile circle's menu and scroll to the last card, Quit (1400 and 900 px; sample data) | A card headed "Quit" with the line "Quit clark when you have finished. Nothing is left running." and one button, "Quit clark". Its link is in the list down the left (or the chips at the top), after Asking. 24 px rule: the button is at least 24 by 24 px. Press it only on the sample copy: the line "Stopping clark. You can close this window." appears and clark stops. |
| S.2 | Settings: look at the Asking card; then open the ask clark panel (1400 and 900 px) | The Asking card has "Claude is started on this computer for you." and, under it, "Your question and the passages it needs go to Claude, which Anthropic runs, and are billed to the Claude account signed in on this computer." The ask clark panel no longer has that sentence above its box. |
| B.1 | Settings > Brands: install an example brand that has a cover picture, then open the Dashboard, Work items, Milestones, Settings and Help (light and dark; 1400 and 900 px) | The brand's picture sits faintly behind the page, under the top bar on the right side, fading out to the left; text stays easy to read. The ask clark panel has the same picture faintly at its foot, behind the box. With no brand, or a brand with no cover picture, nothing is different. |
| B.2 | Help: search for "brand kit" and open "The brand kit guide" (1400 and 900 px) | A guide with seven steps: what a brand kit holds, the limits (50 files, 16 MB the zip, 24 MB unpacked, 5 MB a file), the profile circle, Settings and the Brands card, Choose zip or Review the brand in Downloads, the review, Install, Replace or Cancel, and Undo or Remove. The names it gives (Choose zip, Review the brand in Downloads, Install, Replace, Cancel, Undo, Remove) are on the Brands card. |

| W.2 | Work items: press "+ New view" at the end of the tabs (1400 and 900 px) | A new view appears at once, named "View 1", and is the open tab; no box asks for a name. Press it again: "View 2". Rename view from the tab menu still works and its box says "New name for this view". |

| M.1 | Milestones, the list below the timeline (1400 and 900 px; light and dark) | A light line and a little space (about 14 px above and below the text) between one milestone and the next; no line under the last. The timeline line and its dots stay unbroken. |
