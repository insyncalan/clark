# Hand-check sheet for clark 1.3.12

Written for the person who looks at the screens after the release. One line per thing a person can see that changed; only what a test cannot decide. Hand checks never hold up a release. Sample data only (`bash clark.command --demo`). Chrome at 1400 px, then 900 px. Light and dark where a line says so.

Only full rows (a done-check marker, and the row's PR inside tag v1.3.12-rc1 855d60c9b) are listed. Lines are taken from the row texts and PR titles; I ran nothing.
