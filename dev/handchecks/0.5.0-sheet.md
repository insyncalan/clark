# Hand-check sheet for clark 0.5.0

Written for the person who looks at the screens after the release. Only what a test cannot decide; everything else is covered by the tests. Hand checks never hold up a release.

**Sample data only.** The fictional demo projects. Run `python3 -m clark --demo --open` in the top folder of the release. Laptop only: Chrome at 1400 px wide, then again at 900 px. Light and dark where a line says so (the profile circle, top right, has Dark mode).

Tick each line; write what you saw where it fails.

1. **A published page lets the client filter the work items** (judgement; #69). Publish a demo project (Local only), open the saved file in Chrome and click Work items. You should see All, Actions, Risks and issues, Decisions; Open, Closed, All with counts; a Filter by row; a search box; Group by; and no Client filter or eye icon. Click Closed, All, Open: the list and counts change together. Open one Filter by menu, pick a person, open a second with one click and pick a value: a dot and a chip for each, and a Clear all link. Close the tab and open the file again: your choices are still there. Check once at 900 px. [ ]
2. **A report's Needs from you table reads well** (judgement; #121). In Edit, Add a section, choose Needs from you: a table headed What is needed, From, By. Add a decision, a piece of information and a past date; each row saves in one step and Undo takes it back. In View there are no boxes or buttons and the past-dated row says Overdue as well as showing the colour. Hide a row and Preview: it is missing; an empty section shows nothing on the published page. Check at 900 px: no sideways scroll. [ ]
3. **The peek shows when an item was raised and last reviewed** (judgement; #119, #158). In Work items, click a row: under the properties, Raised and Last reviewed as DD-MM-YYYY, and Not yet where nobody has changed it by hand. On an open item untouched for more than 14 days, a muted chip says "Not reviewed in 14 days" (none on a closed item; write "no sample" if the demo has none). In the Filter by row, Freshness offers "Not reviewed in 14 days" and "No owner or no date"; each shows only matching rows. [ ]
4. **The peek's icons carry their colours** (judgement; #78). Open the peek on an item: the status icon takes its state's colour; priority has its own (Urgent red, High amber, Medium blue, Low grey). A late action has a red date; a late risk, issue or decision and anything due within a few days have an amber date. Switch to dark and look again: nothing is hard to read. [ ]
5. **Word, Excel and the drafted email follow the page's look** (live only; #187). Open a Word copy of a demo report in Word: the same blues, greys and font as the page, not a stock Arial look. Export to Excel (profile menu) and open it: the Cover sheet and header rows use the page's colours and font. Copy a Draft email into a new Outlook message: the colours match the page. [ ]
6. **Adding a section beside another is one step** (judgement; #149). In Edit, Add a section, pick any template and choose beside an existing section: the new one sits next to it. Press Undo once: it is gone and the old one is back to full width. Pick a section that already has a partner: Beside is greyed out with a reason, and nothing is added. [ ]
7. **At 900 px the contents rail becomes an "On this page" button** (judgement; #141). At 1400 px, hover or Tab to the short lines at the right edge: a card headed "On this page" lists every shown section, and a click scrolls there. At 900 px the lines are gone and a pill button "On this page" sits at the bottom right; hover does nothing, a click or Enter or Space opens the list above it, and a second click, Esc or a click elsewhere closes it. It does not cover the last line of the page. Open a published file at 900 px: the same button works. Light and dark are easy to read. [ ]
8. **View draws the text of Current state, Summary, This week and Next week** (judgement; #140). On the dashboard in View, each of the four shows its saved text in plain paragraphs and lists, and a section with nothing saved says "Nothing recorded." (never "Not shown on this page yet."). In Edit the four are unchanged and no error appears. At 900 px and in dark mode the text wraps inside the card and is easy to read. [ ]
9. **Publish shows a spinner, then a tick** (judgement; #130). In Edit, open the profile menu, choose Publish, leave Local ticked and click Publish locally: a small turning circle beside "Publishing…", then a green tick and "Published. Saved locally as ...", with nothing jumping. With Reduce motion on in the Mac's Accessibility settings, the circle does not spin and the tick still arrives. [ ]
10. **On a published page the rows shown always equal the count** (judgement; #138). Publish a demo project, open the file, click Work items and choose Due then Overdue: the count and the rows agree. Add a second filter (Owner or Status): they still agree and every row fits both. Click Clear all: the full list and its count return. [ ]
11. **A published page has a Use cases tab, for reading only** (judgement; #168). In Edit, put the eye on for at least two use cases and off for one; give one open action a use case and a client message, and another a use case with no client message. Publish (Local only) and open the file at 1400 px: tabs read Dashboard, Work items, Milestones, Use cases, with no Settings. The tab and the page heading should read "Use cases"; if either shows "Use Cases" with a capital C, write it down. Only the eye-on use cases are listed, each a row of reference, title and status; a click opens it in place with its headline, standing text, Next steps and Milestones, with no edit button and no private words. Take the eye off every use case and publish again: there is no Use cases tab. At 900 px, light and dark: rows wrap, no sideways scroll. [ ]
12. **The At a glance tiles fill the row** (judgement; #130, #132). On the dashboard in View at 1400 px, the tiles run edge to edge, the small ones are the same width and Project health is twice as wide as one plus the gap. At 900 px four tiles still sit on one line in Edit and View, a long label may wrap, and nothing scrolls sideways. In Edit, a long label stays clear of the grip and the eye; a hidden tile's tag does not cover its figure; with all but one hidden, that one fills the row. Publish and open the file: the same. Light and dark. [ ]
13. **Passwords and keys in other forms are refused** (live only; #257). At 1400 px, open ask clark and type a question with a made-up password in another language, for example: What is the Passwort: Sommer2026x for the portal? You should see "That looks like a password. It was not sent. Take it out and ask again." and no answer. An ordinary question that uses the word password with no value, for example: What does the password policy risk say? is answered as usual. In Settings, add a source folder that holds a text file named notes-password-Hunter2xyz9.txt with ordinary words in it and ask a question about the folder: the panel counts that file among those left out for safety, and none of its words are in the answer. Check the refusal once at 900 px: the line wraps, no sideways scroll. [ ]
14. **Client Requested Report View** (judgement; #192). Open the new tab beside Use cases at 1400 px with the demo project: the page shows the cover line, the overall progress bar with its milestones, progress by plan, Highlights and Planned for next week side by side, and Risks and delays. Check no internal words, ids or "Not set" placeholders read as if meant for the client, and that a milestone kept off the client report is not named. At 900 px the tab reads "Client report"; light and dark: nothing overlaps. [ ]
