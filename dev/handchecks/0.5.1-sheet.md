# Hand-check sheet for clark 0.5.1

Written for the person who looks at the screens after the release. Only what a test cannot decide; everything else is covered by the tests. Hand checks never hold up a release.

**This is the patch sheet.** 0.5.1 follows 0.5.0 and fixes three things found when the 0.5.0 sheet was walked through: saved filters lost on a tab click (check 16), "Use Cases" with a capital C (check 17) and internal words in the client report (check 18). Lines 1 to 15 are the 0.5.0 lines with their wording corrected to match the build; lines 16 to 18 are new.

**Sample data only.** The fictional demo projects. Run `python3 -m clark --demo --open` in the top folder of the release. Laptop only: Chrome at 1400 px wide, then again at 900 px. Light and dark where a line says so (the profile circle, top right, has Appearance with System, Light and Dark).

Tick each line; write what you saw where it fails.

1. **A published page lets the client filter the work items** (judgement; #69). Publish a demo project (Local only), open the saved file in Chrome and click Work items. You should see All, Actions, Risks and issues, Decisions; Open, Closed, All with counts; a Filter by row; a search box; Group by; and no Client filter or eye icon. Click Closed, All, Open: the list and counts change together. Open one Filter by menu, pick a person, open a second with one click and pick a value: a dot and a chip for each, and a Clear all link. Check once at 900 px. Closing the tab and opening the file again is check 16. [ ]
2. **A report's Needs from you table reads well** (judgement; #121). In Edit, Add a section, choose Needs from you: until it has a row it says "Nothing needed from you yet." and shows no table; with the first row a table appears, headed What is needed, From, By. Add a decision, a piece of information and a past date; each row saves in one step and Undo takes it back. In View there are no boxes or buttons and the past-dated row says Overdue as well as showing the colour. Hide a row and Preview: it is missing; an empty section shows nothing on the published page. Check at 900 px: no sideways scroll. [ ]
3. **The peek shows when an item was raised and last reviewed** (judgement; #119, #158). In Work items, open an item's peek (a plain click only selects the row; double-click it or press Enter): under the properties, Raised and Last reviewed as DD-MM-YYYY, and Not yet where nobody has changed it by hand (write "no sample" if no item in the demo shows it). On an open item untouched for more than 14 days, a muted chip says "Not reviewed in 14 days" (none on a closed item; write "no sample" if the demo has none, as a freshly built demo has nothing that old). In the Filter by row, Freshness offers "Not reviewed in 14 days" and "No owner or no date"; each shows only matching rows. [ ]
4. **The peek's icons carry their colours** (judgement; #78). Open the peek on an item: the status icon takes its state's colour; priority has its own (Urgent red, High amber, Medium indigo, Low grey-blue). A late action has a red date; a late risk, issue or decision and anything due within a few days have an amber date. Switch to dark and look again: nothing is hard to read. [ ]
5. **Word, Excel and the drafted email follow the page's look** (live only; #187). Open a Word copy of a demo report in Word: the same blues and greys as the page, in Arial (Arial is the page's office font on purpose, so Arial is right, not a fault). Export to Excel (profile menu) and open it: the Cover sheet and header rows use the page's colours and Arial. Copy a Draft email into a new Outlook message: the colours match the page. If no screen in this release reaches a Word copy or a Draft email, write "not reachable" for that part and judge the Excel file only. [ ]
6. **Adding a section beside another is one step** (judgement; #149). In Edit, Add a section, pick a template that can be half width and choose beside an existing section: the new one sits next to it. Press Undo once: it is gone and the old one is back to full width. Pick a section that already has a partner: Beside is greyed out with a reason, and nothing is added. Beside is also greyed for a template that cannot be half width. [ ]
7. **At 900 px the contents rail becomes an "On this page" button** (judgement; #141). At 1400 px, hover or Tab to the short lines at the right edge: a card headed "On this page" lists every shown section, and a click scrolls there. At 900 px the lines are gone and a pill button "On this page" sits at the bottom right; hover does nothing, a click or Enter or Space opens the list above it, and a second click, Esc or a click elsewhere closes it. It does not cover the last line of the page. Open a published file at 900 px: the same button works. Light and dark are easy to read. [ ]
8. **View draws the text of Current state, Summary, This week and Next week** (judgement; #140). On the dashboard in View, each of the four shows its saved text in plain paragraphs and lists, and a section with nothing saved says "Nothing recorded." (never "Not shown on this page yet."). In Edit the four are unchanged and no error appears. At 900 px and in dark mode the text wraps inside the card and is easy to read. [ ]
9. **Publish shows a spinner, then a tick** (judgement; #130). In Edit, open the profile menu, choose Publish, leave Local ticked and click Publish locally: a small turning circle beside "Publishing…", then a green tick and "Published. Saved locally as ...", with nothing jumping. With Reduce motion on in the Mac's Accessibility settings, the circle does not spin and the tick still arrives. [ ]
10. **On a published page the rows shown always equal the count** (judgement; #138). Publish a demo project, open the file, click Work items and choose Due then Overdue: the count and the rows agree. Add a second filter (Owner or Status): they still agree and every row fits both. Click Clear all: the full list and its count return. [ ]
11. **A published page has a Use cases tab, for reading only** (judgement; #168). In Edit, put the eye on for at least two use cases and off for one; give one open action a use case and a client message, and another a use case with no client message. Publish (Local only) and open the file at 1400 px: tabs read Dashboard, Work items, Milestones, Use cases, with no Settings. The tab and the page heading read "Use cases" (see check 17 for the wording everywhere). Only the eye-on use cases are listed, each a row of reference, title and status; a click opens it in place with its headline, standing text, Next steps and Milestones, with no edit button and no private words. Take the eye off every use case and publish again: there is no Use cases tab. At 900 px, light and dark: rows wrap, no sideways scroll. [ ]
12. **The At a glance tiles fill the row** (judgement; #130, #132). On the dashboard in View at 1400 px, the tiles run edge to edge, the small ones are the same width and Project health is twice as wide as one plus the gap. At 900 px four tiles still sit on one line in Edit and View, a long label may wrap, and nothing scrolls sideways. In Edit, a long label stays clear of the grip and the eye; a hidden tile's tag does not cover its figure; Project health cannot be hidden, so hide the other tiles in turn and with only Health left it fills the row (the grip and the eye do not apply to Health). Publish and open the file: the same. Light and dark. [ ]
13. **Passwords and keys in other forms are refused** (live only; #257). At 1400 px, open ask clark and type a question with a made-up password in another language, for example: What is the Passwort: Sommer2026x for the portal? You should see "That looks like a password or a key, so it wasn't sent. Take it out and try again." and no answer. An ordinary question that uses the word password with no value, for example: What does the password policy risk say? is answered as usual. In Settings, add a source folder that holds a text file named notes-password-Hunter2xyz9.txt with ordinary words in it and ask a question about the folder: the panel counts that file among those left out for safety, and none of its words are in the answer. Check the refusal once at 900 px: the line wraps, no sideways scroll. [ ]
14. **Client Requested Report View** (judgement; #192). Open the Client report tab beside Use cases at 1400 px with the demo project: the page shows the cover line, the overall progress bar with its milestones, progress by plan, Highlights and Planned for next week side by side, and Risks and delays. A milestone kept off the client report is not named, and the milestone count drops by one. Nothing reads as if it were meant for the author or the team: check 18 lists what must be gone. At 900 px the tab reads "Client report"; every section stacks full width; light and dark: nothing overlaps and nothing scrolls sideways. [ ]
15. **A key or password typed into ask clark is refused and no answer appears** (live only; #253). At 1400 px and 900 px, open ask clark on the demo data and ask a question that contains a made-up password, for example: My password is Sommer2026x, can you summarise the report? You should see "That looks like a password or a key, so it wasn't sent. Take it out and try again." and no answer appears. Then ask an ordinary question and see that it is answered. [ ]
16. **Saved filters on a published page survive a tab click** (judgement; the 0.5.1 fix for #69). Publish a demo project (Local only) and open the saved file in Chrome: it opens on Dashboard. Click Work items, open the Owner menu and pick a person, then pick a Status: a chip for each and a dot on each button. Close the tab and open the file again. It opens on Dashboard; click the Work items tab: the same chips are still there and the count and rows agree with them (before 0.5.1 the click wiped them). Click the other tabs and come back: they are still there. Click Clear all, close the tab and open the file again: no chips. Check at 900 px, light and dark. [ ]
17. **"Use cases" is in sentence case wherever a person reads it** (judgement; the 0.5.1 fix, #383). Look for the words at 1400 px: the tab in Edit, the page heading in Edit, the tab and heading in a published file with the eye on for a use case, the Filter by row on Work items, and any Help or Settings line that names the page. Each reads "Use cases" with a small c. Write down any place that still shows "Use Cases" with a capital C. At 900 px, where the tab may sit in a shorter row: the same words. [ ]
18. **The client report shows nothing meant for the author or the team** (judgement; the 0.5.1 fix, #192). Open the Client report tab with the demo project at 1400 px and read every line. There must be no row ids (such as a letter code and number) beside the risks and delays; no Escalate line; no box saying a template is not built yet; no "Nothing written yet" line that tells the author where to write; and no "Not set" in the client's view. A section with nothing to say should read as quiet or be left out, not as an instruction. Milestones, progress and the cover line still show. Repeat at 900 px, light and dark: the same. [ ]
