# Design system: components clark adds

The entries for the parts of clark that the main design system (`docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`, section 5) does not describe yet. Each entry says what the part is, how it looks (tokens only, from the committed look), its states, what happens at 900 px, and what is not settled. A person's own look (`system/branding/`) only changes token values, so none of these entries names a brand, a logo or a picture.

Every entry is checked on the live page at 1400 and 900 px, in light and dark, against the checks in section 7 of the main design system: text 4.5 to 1, controls and rings 3 to 1, text at least 13 px, targets at least 24 px, no sideways scroll of the page. "Not yet checked" is written where a check has not been made. The 'text under 12 px' figures in the entries were measured with the 12 px caption size as the floor, which is what the measuring script reports; text between 12 and 13 px has not been measured separately against that 13 px floor, so each entry lists it under 'Not yet checked'.

**How the figures were taken.** Each 'Measured' line comes from a headless browser run over the open page with fictional demo data: a script reads the page's own colours and sizes and reports text below 4.5 to 1 contrast, text under 12 px, controls under 24 px, elements that overflow their box and sideways scroll of the page. The board, Calendar, Legend and timeline were measured on the 0.2.1 release (the v0.2.1 tag); the profile menu on the branch of the Appearance and name-and-email change. The figures are repeatable with the same script; they are not part of the test suite.

**Status of the set.** This is the second batch: ten entries in all (sections 1 to 10). The rest are still to write: the peek and Sneak peek, ask clark (panel, bubbles, chips), the AI suggestion card, the Add tiles and the growing Add button, the Forecast state, the new Use cases page (section 10 covers only the use case rows on the Dashboard), editor tables against the report table, dark mode, empty states, keyboard shortcuts, and tooltips. Phone width is out of scope for now (laptop only).

## 1. The Work items board
Cards in lanes, with the lanes grouped by status, due date, priority or another field.
- **Look.** A card is `--surface` with a one-pixel `--grey-200` edge, `--radius` and `--shadow`; padding 10 by 12 px. Top line: the item's id in `--text-muted` (13 px), the move button on the right. Title: 14 px, at most three lines, then cut. Foot: the due date and the status chip (tint, dot and word) on the right. A finished card's title is `--text-muted`. A selected card has a `--brand-blue` edge. A lane heading is 13 px `--text-muted`.
- **Colour.** Status, due and priority lanes carry the colour of their state on the heading. The word is always there: colour is never the only cue.
- **States.** Rest; hover (the move button goes from faint to full); focus (2 px `--brand-blue` ring, offset 2 px, on the card's main area and on the move button); selected; being dragged (half opacity). In Edit a card can be dragged; in View it cannot.
- **Move button.** 24 px square, 16 px icon, faint at rest and full on hover or focus: the keyboard way to move a card.
- **900 px.** The lanes scroll sideways inside the board; the page itself never scrolls sideways. The board is the designed exception to "no horizontal scroll at 900 px". The headings stay pinned under the top bar while the board scrolls.
- **Measured** (light and dark, both widths): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** The focus ring on a card, the disabled and error states, reduced motion on the drag, and the peek opened from the board, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** The lane headings sit under the page title with no level in between (a heading-order gap). A column that does not fit at 900 px has no cue that it is there. In full screen the top bar is hidden, so the tab bar goes with it.

## 2. The profile menu
The round button at the right of the top bar and its menu.
- **Status of this entry.** It describes the profile menu as built for the Appearance and name-and-email change, which is on main. The 'Measured' line was taken on the branch before it merged.
- **Structure, top to bottom.** The person's name in bold with the email under it (or only the name), or the link "Add your name in Settings" when neither is set; the actions (Publish, Preview the page in a new tab, Export to Excel); a separator; the group named Appearance with System, Light and Dark, the one in use ticked; a separator; Settings and Help.
- **Look.** A menu panel on `--surface`, `--radius-lg`, `--shadow`; rows with a hover and keyboard highlight; the tick is `--status-blue-text` in a fixed 1.2 em box so the words line up; the group name is a quiet 13 px `--text-muted` line.
- **For a screen reader.** The three Appearance choices are radio items inside one group named Appearance, so each is read as "Appearance, Light, radio, checked".
- **Keys.** Arrow keys, Home and End move between rows; Enter chooses; Escape closes and puts focus back on the round button.
- **900 px.** Long names and emails are cut with an ellipsis and the full text is the tooltip; the menu keeps to 280 px for them.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, controls under 24 px 0, text under 12 px 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** Disabled rows (Publish and Export wait until the report has loaded), reduced motion, and the focus ring on every row, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** "Preview the page in a new tab" wraps to two lines at 200 px; the menu needs a stated minimum width.

## 3. The milestone Calendar (Week, Month, Quarter)
- **Look.** Seven columns divided by one-pixel `--grey-200` lines. Weekend and out-of-month days sit on `--grey-50`. Today's number is a filled `--brand-blue` pill with `--text-on-brand`. Day numbers are 13 px `--text-muted`. A milestone in a day is a small row (14 px icon and the name, cut with an ellipsis); a milestone whose date has moved is drawn as a dashed `--status-red` outline with red text and no fill.
- **Scales.** A button group switches Week, Month and Quarter; the one in use has `aria-current`, a tinted fill and a `--brand-blue` edge. Day heights: Week 180 px, Month 88 px, Quarter 44 px with the name hidden (icon only, three months across).
- **States.** Hover tints the milestone (`--brand-blue-tint`); focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring on a milestone and on the scale buttons. In a published file a milestone is text, not a button: no hover, default cursor.
- **900 px.** Day height 64 px; names are hidden (icons only); the Quarter view becomes one column. A name hidden at 900 px is reachable only by opening the milestone.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** Reduced motion on the scale change, arrow keys between days, and the contrast of the dashed moved outline on a tinted day, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** Whether a hidden name at 900 px should get a tooltip.

## 4. The milestone Legend
- **Look.** A quiet button at the right above the timeline: icon and word in `--text-muted` 13 px, at least 28 px high and wide. It opens a panel (320 px, or the window width less 48 px) on `--surface` with a `--grey-200` edge, `--radius-lg` and `--shadow`: a row for each marker with a 20 px symbol and the words, and a second block below a rule for the symbols (the moved outline, the Today line).
- **States.** Opens on hover or keyboard focus and closes when both leave; Escape shuts it until hover or focus moves on. Hover turns the button text from `--text-muted` to `--text`; focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring. The panel is not a dialog: it holds no controls and does not trap focus.
- **Motion.** The panel fades and moves 4 px; reduced motion is not yet checked.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0.
- **Open points.** The panel opens upward, so near the top of the page it can leave the window: check at 900 px with the page at the top. Opening on hover is a mouse habit; the keyboard opens it by focus.

## 5. Milestone markers and Movement (the timeline)
- **Look.** A strip on `--grey-50` between two `--grey-200` rules; month lines with 13 px `--text-muted` names; a 2 px `--grey-300` line through the middle; Today is a 2 px `--brand-navy` line with the word "Today" in `--status-blue-text` on a `--surface` chip. A marker is a 24 px round target with a 16 px icon, scaling to 1.18 on hover or focus. A label is the name in medium weight with the date under it, drawn above the Today line on the strip's own colour so no line is drawn through a name or a date. Green label text means on time; red means moved or late, and the words say it too.
- **Moved milestone.** A 14 px dashed `--status-red` ghost circle at the old date and a dashed red connector to the new one: shapes, not only colour.
- **States.** Hover tints the label; focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring, offset 2 px. In a published file a marker is text: no hover, default cursor.
- **Scroll.** The strip scrolls sideways inside its own box and can be made taller (up to 900 px); the page does not scroll sideways.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** Reduced motion on the hover scale, and two labels within a few days of each other, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** There is no rule yet for two close labels (stack them, hide one, or collide).

## 6. The filter bar and its pills (Work items)
The two rows above the table or the board: the switches, then "Filter by" with one pill for each field, then the count line with a chip for each filter that is on.
- **Structure.** Row one holds two switches (All, Actions, Risks and issues, Decisions; then Open, Closed, All, each with its count) and, at the right, the Group by menu and the Table or Board switch. Row two is the quiet 13 px `--text-muted` words "Filter by" and a pill for Workstream, Status, Due, Owner, Priority, Use case, Milestone and Client (Freshness is added only when some item carries a date it can use). Under them: the count ("9 open items"), a chip for each filter that is on, and "Clear all" (only when something differs from the start: a filter, a type other than All, or Closed or All). When nothing matches, the list says "No items match these filters." with a Clear all button.
- **The switches.** A pill-shaped group on `--surface` with a one-pixel `--border` edge; buttons are 14 px and at least 32 px high; the count after the word is `--text-muted`. The one in use is filled with `--pill-bg`, text `--pill-fg`, bold; hover is `--grey-50`. Each button says which is on with `aria-pressed`.
- **A filter pill.** 13 px `--text-muted`, at least 28 px high, padding 3 by 10 px, a 14 px chevron, `--radius-pill`, no visible edge. Hover and open: `--grey-100` fill and `--text`. A filter that is on turns its word `--status-blue-text` and gains a 6 px `--brand-blue` dot at the top right; its accessible name adds ": filtered".
- **The filter box.** Opens under its pill: 260 px wide, up to 340 px high and then scrolls, `--surface`, one-pixel `--border` edge, `--radius-lg`, `--shadow`, 14 px text. At the top a two-pill choice "is any of" and "is not" (13 px; the one in use is `--status-blue-text` on `--brand-blue-tint`) and the small buttons Select all and Clear. Then one row for each value: a tick box, the word, and its count at the right in 13 px `--text-muted`; hover is `--grey-50`. When it would run off the window it turns to open from the pill's right edge.
- **Chips and Clear all.** A chip is 13 px `--status-blue-text` on `--brand-blue-tint`, pill-shaped, with the filter written out ("Status: Cancelled", "Owner is not: ...") and a round 22 px remove button (hover `--grey-200`) that says "Remove the Status filter". Clear all is a text button, `--status-blue-text`, 14 px, 24 px high.
- **States.** Rest; hover; open (`aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the pill); filter on (dot and colour); focus. Opening a box puts focus on its first tick box, and a tick takes effect at once. The controls are never redrawn by a filter change, only their words and pressed states, so a second click lands first time. Escape closes the box and puts focus back on its pill; a click outside closes it; moving focus away closes it.
- **Focus rings.** 2 px `--brand-blue`: offset 1 px on pills, 2 px on Select all, Clear and Clear all, 1 px on the "is any of" and "is not" pills, and inside the button (offset -2 px) on the switches. The tick boxes in the box have no rule of their own and show the browser's ring.
- **For a screen reader.** The bar is a group named "Filter by"; the box is a group named for its field (for example "Filter by Owner"); the note line for "that filter would not fit in the page's address" is a status line; a hidden live line announces grouping changes. The count line is not a live region.
- **900 px.** The same. All eight pills still fit on one row (834 px wide); on the switch row the Group by menu and the Table or Board switch drop to a second line. The box keeps its 260 px.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark; bar closed, one box open, and two filters on): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, no sideways page scroll. Controls under 24 px: the chips' remove button is 22 by 22 px in all four runs; every other control is 24 px or more (pills 28, switches 33, Clear all 24). Focus ring against the surface: 6.29 to 1 in light and 4.44 to 1 in dark; the tick boxes' own ring 5.98 to 1 and 8.31 to 1.
- **Not yet checked.** The focus ring on the pressed switch in dark (worked out from the token values at about 2 to 1 against the pill fill, not measured on the page); the box at its right-edge turn; a filter that cannot be added; reduced motion; the box with a long list of values; the Freshness pill, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** The chip's remove button is 2 px short of the 24 px target (`.fchip button`; the rule that raises it to 24 px applies only at phone width). The tick boxes in the box are the browser's 13 px default with no `accent-color`, unlike the 16 px ticks elsewhere (see section 7). The "filter on" cue is a colour and a dot only; the chips below say it in words. The count line is not announced when it changes.

## 7. Form controls
Text boxes, tick boxes, radio buttons and buttons as used in Settings, the editors, the Publish dialog and the pickers. There are no drop-down selects in the page.
- **Text box.** `--surface` fill, a one-pixel `--grey-300` edge, `--radius`, 15 px `--text`, at least 32 px high, padding 4 by 10 px. The variants: the owner box in a cell's picker has `--radius-sm` and padding 4 by 8 px (33 px high measured); the Help search box is 36 px high, 300 px wide; the board's date prompt and the writing editor's link box use `--radius-sm`; the project health reason is a taller box (at least 62 px, grows downward) with `--radius-sm`. Section titles are text in place: a transparent edge that shows `--grey-300` on hover.
- **Tick boxes and radio buttons.** 16 px, with `accent-color: var(--brand-blue)`, in Settings, each with its words and a quiet 13 px `--text-muted` line under the words. In the Publish dialog the whole option is a row with a one-pixel `--grey-200` edge and `--radius`; it gets a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring (offset 1 px) when its tick box has focus.
- **Buttons.** The standard button: `--surface`, one-pixel `--border`, pill-shaped, 14 px, at least 32 px high, padding 6 by 18 px; hover darkens the edge to `--grey-500`. Main action (`pri`): `--brand-blue` fill with `--text-on-brand`. Quiet: no fill. Small: 13 px, padding 2 by 10 px. The main action elsewhere (Try again, Accept) is the committed look's pill: `--pill-bg` with `--pill-fg`, bold, 38 px high, a soft `--pill-glow` that grows on hover with a 1 px lift. A text button is `--status-blue-text`, 14 px, at least 24 by 24 px, underlined on hover.
- **Focus.** A 2 px ring in `--brand-blue` (`--focus` in the editors: the same colour in the committed tokens): offset 2 px on buttons, 1 px on text boxes and 0 px on the owner box. Tick boxes and radio buttons in Settings and the filter box have no rule and show the browser's own ring.
- **Error line.** Under the box, 13 px `--status-red-text`, `role="alert"`, saying what is wrong and what to do ("An owner can't be blank. Type a name, or TBC if no one is known yet."). The box keeps its grey edge. Messages in Publish and Settings use the same red for an error and `--status-green-text` for success, in a line that is a status line (an alert for an error). A health reason that is needed gets a `--status-amber` edge.
- **Disabled.** A disabled button has its opacity lowered (0.6; 0.55 for a main action) and the default cursor. A disabled tick box is the browser's greyed box (the Publish option that is always on; the To client option until a client folder is set, whose row text turns `--text-muted`).
- **900 px.** Nothing changes for the controls themselves; the dialog's padding goes from 20 by 24 to 16 px.
- **Measured** (Settings in Edit, an owner box open, the Publish dialog and a filter box open; both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, targets under 24 px 0 (the page check skips tick boxes and radio buttons; read by hand they are 16 px, and 13 px in the filter box, each inside a row that is 33 px or more high), no sideways page scroll. The ring on a text box is 6.29 to 1 against the surface in light and 4.44 to 1 in dark; the browser's ring on tick boxes 5.98 to 1 and 8.31 to 1. Error line shown and checked at 1400 px light only.
- **Not yet checked.** The error line in dark and at 900 px; the rename box and the overwrite tick in Publish (they appear only when a file name clashes); the disabled look of the picker buttons; the health reason box with the amber edge; reduced motion for the main button's lift (the CSS removes it); a screen reader on the boxes' labels, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** The Keys group in Settings has no rule: it shows the browser's own groove edge (light on dark in dark mode) and a 15 px legend in `--text`, where the Section style group above it has no edge and a 13 px `--text-muted` legend (`fieldset.keys`). Three tick box looks exist (accent colour 16 px; browser default 16 px; browser default 13 px). A box with an error does not change its own edge, so the cue is the red line only. Rings differ in offset (0, 1 and 2 px). In the Publish dialog the main button is 48 px high against 34 px for Cancel; the cause is not yet looked into. The Add from your project record panel is written (`Picker.jsx`) but nothing on the page opens it yet, so it was not measured.

## 8. Dialogs and toasts
The Publish dialog and the other panels that open over the page, and the message that says what the last change did or what went wrong.
- **The Publish dialog.** A native dialog opened as a modal: the page behind is dimmed with a black backdrop at 40% and cannot be reached. Width: 520 px, or the window less 32 px if that is smaller (520 px at both widths); centred; `--surface`, a one-pixel `--border` edge, `--radius-lg`, `--shadow`; padding 20 by 24 px (16 px at 900 px). Top to bottom: the title (`--size-h2`, `--heading`; it names the dialog), a 13 px `--text-muted` line, an optional box of things to check with a "Show them" text button, the options as bordered rows, a message line (at least 20 px high), and the buttons at the right: Cancel (quiet) and the main action, whose words say what will happen ("Publish locally", "Publish and send to the client", "Close", "OK"). It also asks one question when a file name clashes (Add a number, Rename it, Overwrite the existing file).
- **Keys and focus.** Focus starts on the To client tick when it can be ticked, otherwise on the main button; on the chosen answer when the question is asked. Tab stays inside; Escape cancels, and focus goes back to the round profile button (checked). The message line is `role="status"`, and an alert when it is an error: red `--status-red-text`, green `--status-green-text` for success, `--text-muted` for a note.
- **Other panels.** The pickers in a Work items cell (Status, Priority, Due, Owner) are one small panel, `role="dialog"`: 180 px at least, 320 px at most, `--surface`, `--border`, `--radius`, `--shadow`; under the cell or above it when there is no room, and moved to fit the window. Escape closes it and leaves the value as it was; so does a click outside; Tab closes it and carries on. The board's due date and decision prompts are `role="dialog"` panels with their error line as an alert. The panels for adding from the project record (640 px) and drafting an email (760 px) are written but not opened from the page yet.
- **The change toast.** Edit only. A bar fixed at the bottom left, 16 px from the edges, at most 640 px wide or the window less 32 px; `--brand-navy` fill, white 15 px text, `--radius`, `--shadow`, padding 10 by 14 px, a 4 px left edge in `--brand-cyan`. It says what changed in a sentence, then the buttons Undo (only while there is a step to undo) and Close: 13 px, underlined, white, 28 px high, hover a faint white fill, focus a 2 px `--brand-cyan` ring. Undo replaces the toast with one that says the change is back, with Close only (checked).
- **The problem toast.** The same bar with the left edge in `--status-red` and `role="alert"`; it says what went wrong and what to do in plain words, and has Close only.
- **A note.** The same bar as a change toast without Undo, for a short word on what Preview or Export did, or why a move was not made.
- **How long.** A change toast or note goes after 20 seconds (checked: still there at 19, gone at 22); a problem stays until Close or the next message. One toast at a time: a new one replaces the old. Escape does not close a toast (checked). The change and problem toasts show only in Edit. The wrapper is a polite status region that is always in the page in Edit, so a screen reader hears each message.
- **900 px.** The dialog keeps 520 px and its padding is 16 px; the toast keeps its place and width (441 px and 601 px for two sentences, 48 px high).
- **Measured** (the Publish dialog on the choose step, a change toast and a problem toast; both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, no sideways page scroll. Rings: on the dialog's buttons 6.29 to 1 light and 4.44 to 1 dark against `--surface`; on the toast's buttons 8.85 to 1 and 10.49 to 1 against the toast.
- **Not yet checked.** The Publish dialog while working, after an error, when done, and with the file name question; the date and decision prompts; reduced motion (the CSS gives the dialog and the toast no animation of their own); the dialog at a small window height; a toast with a very long sentence; two toasts in quick succession read by a screen reader, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** The toast sits over the lowest content at the left (for example the foot of the timeline) and there is no way to pause the 20-second timer. The dialog's title is the first heading in the page and comes before the page's own heading, so the heading-order check reports a gap while it is open. The Publish main button is taller than Cancel (48 and 34 px).

## 9. The Help view
A panel that replaces the open page, with three tabs, a search box, entries, a table of keys, and entries held back until the part of clark they describe is on the page.
- **How it opens.** Press `?` anywhere outside a box, dialog or menu (or choose Help in the profile menu). It is a layer over the page: the address, the project and the view stay, and Escape or Close Help puts the person back; moving to another page or project closes it. It exists only on a page clark serves (not in a published file). It opens on the tab used last, which is remembered on the computer.
- **Header.** The title (the page's top heading), one line saying how to open and close it with `?` drawn as a key cap, a search box (300 px wide, 36 px high) and a Close Help button (36 px high), both with `--radius`, a one-pixel `--grey-300` edge.
- **Tabs.** Start here, How do I..., Reference: a tab list 43 px high, 15 px; the chosen tab is `--heading` in medium weight with a 3 px `--brand-blue` bar under it, the others `--text-muted` (hover `--text`). Arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs (checked: the right arrow moved focus and chose the next tab); only the chosen tab is in the tab order.
- **How do I... and Reference.** Two columns: a contents list at the left (230 px, stays in view while the page scrolls; group names in 13 px capitals `--text-muted`, links 14 px with `--grey-100` on hover) and the entries at the right. An entry is a card on `--surface` with a one-pixel `--grey-200` edge, `--radius-lg`, padding 18 by 22 px; the title is 18 px `--heading`; a "Where:" line in 13 px `--text-muted`; numbered steps; a note with a 3 px `--brand-blue` left edge on `--brand-blue-tint`; and a text link at the foot that takes the person to the screen ("Open Work items ›"). A reference entry holds paragraphs and tables: column heads 13 px capitals `--text-muted`, rows 14 px divided by `--grey-100`.
- **The key table.** Two columns, Keys and Does. Keys are drawn as key caps (13 px, a one-pixel `--grey-300` edge with a thicker foot, radius 4 px, `--surface`); the first column does not wrap; the modifier shows as the Command symbol on a Mac and Ctrl otherwise.
- **Search.** One box searches all three tabs; the results replace the tab: a line "2 results" (14 px `--text-muted`), group names in 13 px capitals `--status-blue-text`, and each result as a link card (title and the matching line) with a `--brand-blue` edge and a 3 px `--brand-blue-tint` glow on hover or focus. Enter opens the first result; the down arrow moves into the list; `/` returns to the box (when single-key shortcuts are on). With no result: "Nothing in Help for ..." and "Try another word, or look through the tabs." Escape clears the search first, then the next Escape closes Help (checked). The results are a polite live region.
- **Landing.** A link inside Help scrolls to the entry and flashes it with `--flash` for 2.4 seconds; with reduced motion there is a 2 px `--status-amber` outline instead of a fade.
- **Held-back entries.** The words live in one source. A second list holds back entries for parts of clark that are not on the page yet: a held entry leaves the tabs, the contents, the search and the links, a group with nothing left goes too, and a tab with nothing left shows "Nothing here yet. This appears when the part of clark it describes is ready." in `--text-muted`. Links to screens not on the page yet are drawn as plain words. A third list keeps wording taken out for review; it is shown nowhere. In this version: Start here shows none of its seven parts, How do I... shows 1 entry of 26, Reference shows 1 of 30, and the key table shows 6 of 21 rows.
- **900 px.** No change to the layout: the two columns stay (230 px and 548 px), the intro line takes two lines, and the page does not scroll sideways.
- **Measured** (Start here, How do I..., Reference with the key table, and a search with results; both widths, light and dark; a search with no result at 1400 px light only): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, no sideways page scroll. Controls under 24 px: the link at the foot of an entry ("Open Work items ›") is 121 by 21 px in all four runs of How do I... Heading order: the search results go from the top heading straight to third-level group names, in all four runs. Rings: 2 px `--brand-blue` on the search box, Close Help, the tabs, the contents links and the results (6.29 to 1 light, 4.44 to 1 dark); the panel itself takes focus when it opens and shows the browser's ring (5.53 to 1 and 9.36 to 1).
- **Not yet checked.** The parts of Start here (hero, the loop, first steps, jobs, screens, rules, foot): they are held, so none is drawn; the icon table and the milestone marks table; reduced motion on the flash; where focus lands when Help closes; the contents list at a short window height; a screen reader on the tabs, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** The link at the foot of an entry is 3 px short of a 24 px target (`.hp-go`). Search result group names are third-level headings with no second level above them (`.hp-resg h3`). While a search shows, the tab panel is left out of the page, but each tab still names it with `aria-controls`. The panel takes the browser's own focus ring rather than the 2 px `--brand-blue` ring. With nearly everything held, Help is mostly empty today: when held entries are released, every tab needs measuring again.

## 10. Use case rows on the Dashboard
The use case summary: a strip, then one slim row for each use case; a row opens in place. This is how it is on the page today (the new Use cases page is a later entry).
- **The strip.** The total in medium weight `--heading` ("3 use cases", "1 use case"); then, for each status that has any, a dot and a count ("1 On track", "1 Flag", "1 High risk") in `--text`; then a meter 8 px high and up to 280 px wide, with one block (2 px radius) for each use case, coloured `--status-green`, `--status-amber` or `--status-red` (a use case whose status word is none of the three is in the total but not in the counts or the meter). The meter is an image with a text label that gives the same counts.
- **A row.** A grid with fixed columns: the reference (56 px, `--text-muted`), the title (flexible, medium weight, `--heading`), the headline (flexible, `--text-muted`), the status (104 px) and the next steps (112 px, 13 px `--text-muted`: "1 next step", "3 next steps", "No open steps"). At least 40 px high, padding 6 by 4 px, a one-pixel `--grey-100` rule between rows and a `--grey-200` rule above the first. Because the columns are fixed, the left edges of the status words are equal in every row (checked: all at the same x).
- **The status word.** An 8 px round dot (hidden from a screen reader) and the word, in `--text`: On track (`--status-green`), Flag (`--status-amber`), High risk (`--status-red`), grey for a word that is none of them. The word is always there, so colour is never the only cue.
- **A row that opens.** Click anywhere on the row, or press Enter or Space. It opens in place (several can be open at once; the choice is not saved) with the detail indented 72 px under the title: "Where it stands" (13 px capitals `--text-muted`, then the saved text as plain text, or "Nothing written yet."), "Next steps" (one line each as "Owner: what it is", the owner in bold; or "No open next steps."), and a text button "See all 2 open work items", which opens Work items filtered to that use case (checked). A use case with no reference shows the count as plain words, and with none open "No open work items."
- **States.** Rest; hover (`--grey-50` fill); focus (2 px `--brand-blue` ring drawn inside the row, offset -2 px); open (`aria-expanded` true). The row is a button by role, in the tab order; Space opened it and Enter closed it (checked). With no use cases the section says "No use cases yet."
- **For a screen reader.** The row is `role="button"` and says whether it is open; it names the open detail with `aria-controls`, but that detail exists only while the row is open. The meter is an image with a label.
- **900 px.** The columns become 44 px, flexible, flexible, 96 px and 96 px with an 8 px gap, the detail is indented 52 px, and a long headline wraps to a second line, so rows grow.
- **Measured** (rows closed, and one row open; both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, no sideways page scroll. Ring against the surface: 6.29 to 1 in light, 4.44 to 1 in dark. The "See all" button is 24 px high.
- **Not yet checked.** More than three use cases or very long titles; reduced motion (the CSS gives the rows no animation); how a screen reader reads a row (its name is all the words in it joined); a published file, where the counts and the text come from the file; a status word that is none of the three, and text between 12 and 13 px against the 13 px floor.
- **Open points.** The amber dot and the amber meter block are 2.2 to 1 against white (the green 4.5 and red 5.4 pass 3 to 1); they are not controls and the word beside the dot carries the status, but the meter has no word under each block. A row's accessible name is long. `aria-controls` points at nothing while the row is closed.
