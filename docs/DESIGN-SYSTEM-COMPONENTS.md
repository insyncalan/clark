# Design system: components clark adds

The entries for the parts of clark that the main design system (`docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`, section 5) does not describe yet. Each entry says what the part is, how it looks (tokens only, from the committed look), its states, what happens at 900 px, and what is not settled. A person's own look (`system/branding/`) only changes token values, so none of these entries names a brand, a logo or a picture.

Every entry is checked on the live page at 1400 and 900 px, in light and dark, against the checks in section 7 of the main design system: text 4.5 to 1, controls and rings 3 to 1, text at least 12 px, targets at least 24 px, no sideways scroll of the page. "Not yet checked" is written where a check has not been made.

**How the figures were taken.** Each 'Measured' line comes from a headless browser run over the open page with fictional demo data: a script reads the page's own colours and sizes and reports text below 4.5 to 1 contrast, text under 12 px, controls under 24 px, elements that overflow their box and sideways scroll of the page. The board, Calendar, Legend and timeline were measured on the 0.2.1 release (the v0.2.1 tag); the profile menu on the branch of the Appearance and name-and-email change. The figures are repeatable with the same script; they are not part of the test suite.

**Status of the set.** This is the first batch: five entries. The rest are still to write: the peek and Sneak peek, ask clark (panel, bubbles, chips), the AI suggestion card, the Add tiles and the growing Add button, the Forecast state, the filter bar and its pills, form controls, dialogs and toasts, the Help view, use case rows (and the new Use cases page), editor tables against the report table, dark mode, empty states, keyboard shortcuts, and tooltips. Phone width is out of scope for now (laptop only).

## 1. The Work items board
Cards in lanes, with the lanes grouped by status, due date, priority or another field.
- **Look.** A card is `--surface` with a one-pixel `--grey-200` edge, `--radius` and `--shadow`; padding 10 by 12 px. Top line: the item's id in `--text-muted` (13 px), the move button on the right. Title: 14 px, at most three lines, then cut. Foot: the due date and the status chip (tint, dot and word) on the right. A finished card's title is `--text-muted`. A selected card has a `--brand-blue` edge. A lane heading is 13 px `--text-muted`.
- **Colour.** Status, due and priority lanes carry the colour of their state on the heading. The word is always there: colour is never the only cue.
- **States.** Rest; hover (the move button goes from faint to full); focus (2 px `--brand-blue` ring, offset 2 px, on the card's main area and on the move button); selected; being dragged (half opacity). In Edit a card can be dragged; in View it cannot.
- **Move button.** 24 px square, 16 px icon, faint at rest and full on hover or focus: the keyboard way to move a card.
- **900 px.** The lanes scroll sideways inside the board; the page itself never scrolls sideways. The board is the designed exception to "no horizontal scroll at 900 px". The headings stay pinned under the top bar while the board scrolls.
- **Measured** (light and dark, both widths): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** The focus ring on a card, the disabled and error states, reduced motion on the drag, and the peek opened from the board.
- **Open points.** The lane headings sit under the page title with no level in between (a heading-order gap). A column that does not fit at 900 px has no cue that it is there. In full screen the top bar is hidden, so the tab bar goes with it.

## 2. The profile menu
The round button at the right of the top bar and its menu.
- **Status of this entry.** It describes the profile menu as built for the Appearance and name-and-email items (#179 and #180), which is not on main yet; it will be true when that lands. On main today the menu holds Publish, Preview and Export, a Dark mode item, Settings and Help. Read the structure, the screen-reader wording, the 900 px rule and the keys below as the target for that change, and check them against the page once it is merged.
- **Structure, top to bottom.** The person's name in bold with the email under it (or only the name), or the link "Add your name in Settings" when neither is set; the actions (Publish, Preview the page in a new tab, Export to Excel); a separator; the group named Appearance with System, Light and Dark, the one in use ticked; a separator; Settings and Help.
- **Look.** A menu panel on `--surface`, `--radius-lg`, `--shadow`; rows with a hover and keyboard highlight; the tick is `--status-blue-text` in a fixed 1.2 em box so the words line up; the group name is a quiet 13 px `--text-muted` line.
- **For a screen reader.** The three Appearance choices are radio items inside one group named Appearance, so each is read as "Appearance, Light, radio, checked".
- **Keys.** Arrow keys, Home and End move between rows; Enter chooses; Escape closes and puts focus back on the round button.
- **900 px.** Long names and emails are cut with an ellipsis and the full text is the tooltip; the menu keeps to 280 px for them.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, controls under 24 px 0, text under 12 px 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** Disabled rows (Publish and Export wait until the report has loaded), reduced motion, and the focus ring on every row.
- **Open points.** "Preview the page in a new tab" wraps to two lines at 200 px; the menu needs a stated minimum width.

## 3. The milestone Calendar (Week, Month, Quarter)
- **Look.** Seven columns divided by one-pixel `--grey-200` lines. Weekend and out-of-month days sit on `--grey-50`. Today's number is a filled `--brand-blue` pill with `--text-on-brand`. Day numbers are 13 px `--text-muted`. A milestone in a day is a small row (14 px icon and the name, cut with an ellipsis); a milestone whose date has moved is drawn as a dashed `--status-red` outline with red text and no fill.
- **Scales.** A button group switches Week, Month and Quarter; the one in use has `aria-current`, a tinted fill and a `--brand-blue` edge. Day heights: Week 180 px, Month 88 px, Quarter 44 px with the name hidden (icon only, three months across).
- **States.** Hover tints the milestone (`--brand-blue-tint`); focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring on a milestone and on the scale buttons. In a published file a milestone is text, not a button: no hover, default cursor.
- **900 px.** Day height 64 px; names are hidden (icons only); the Quarter view becomes one column. A name hidden at 900 px is reachable only by opening the milestone.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** Reduced motion on the scale change, arrow keys between days, and the contrast of the dashed moved outline on a tinted day.
- **Open points.** Whether a hidden name at 900 px should get a tooltip.

## 4. The milestone Legend
- **Look.** A quiet button at the right above the timeline: icon and word in `--text-muted` 13 px, at least 28 px high and wide. It opens a panel (320 px, or the window width less 48 px) on `--surface` with a `--grey-200` edge, `--radius-lg` and `--shadow`: a row for each marker with a 20 px symbol and the words, and a second block below a rule for the symbols (the moved outline, the Today line).
- **States.** Opens on hover or keyboard focus and closes when both leave; Escape shuts it until hover or focus moves on. Hover turns the button text from `--text-muted` to `--text`; focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring. The panel is not a dialog: it holds no controls and does not trap focus.
- **Motion.** The panel fades and moves 4 px; reduced motion is not yet checked.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0.
- **Open points.** The panel opens upward, so near the top of the page it can leave the window: check at 900 px with the page at the top. Opening on hover is a mouse habit; the keyboard opens it by focus.

## 5. Milestone markers and Movement (the timeline)
- **Look.** A strip on `--grey-50` between two `--grey-200` rules; month lines with 13 px `--text-muted` names; a 2 px `--grey-300` line through the middle; Today is a 2 px `--brand-navy` line with the word "Today" in `--status-blue-text` on a `--surface` chip. A marker is a 24 px round target with a 16 px icon, scaling to 1.18 on hover or focus. A label is the name in medium weight with the date under it, drawn above the Today line on the strip's own colour so no line is drawn through a name or a date. Green label text means on time; red means moved or late, and the words say it too.
- **Moved milestone.** A 14 px dashed `--status-red` ghost circle at the old date and a dashed red connector to the new one: shapes, not only colour.
- **States.** Hover tints the label; focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring, offset 2 px. In a published file a marker is text: no hover, default cursor.
- **Scroll.** The strip scrolls sideways inside its own box and can be made taller (up to 900 px); the page does not scroll sideways.
- **Measured** (both widths, light and dark): contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** Reduced motion on the hover scale, and two labels within a few days of each other.
- **Open points.** There is no rule yet for two close labels (stack them, hide one, or collide).

## 11. The Add button and the section Add controls
The "+" that grows into its words on Work items, the "+" under a section's grip on the Dashboard in Edit, and the "Add a section" bar of a report with no sections. There is no Add tile: on main the Dashboard's tiles have only a grip and an eye in Edit, and the Add button is on Work items only (Milestones and Use cases have none yet).
- **Look: the Add button.** 32 px square at rest, `--radius`, fill `--brand-blue-tint`, an 18 px plus icon (stroke 2.2) in `--status-blue-text`, padding 0 7 px. On hover, keyboard focus or while its menu is open the fill turns `--brand-blue`, the text `--text-on-brand`, and the button grows to show its words in 14 px medium weight (the word's width goes from 0 to at most 180 px, with 6 px before it and 4 px after). "Add item" measured 115 px wide when grown. A button with a menu also shows a 14 px chevron when grown.
- **Words.** They follow the type switch on Work items: Add item (All, with a menu of Add action, Add risk, Add issue and Add decision), Add action, Add risk or issue, Add decision. The accessible name, the `title` and the styled tip always hold the full words, so the button is named at rest.
- **Look: the menu.** `.ddmenu`: `--surface`, `--border` edge, `--radius-lg`, `--shadow`, at least 200 px wide, rows of 14 px text with 8 by 14 px padding; hover and keyboard focus fill a row with `--brand-blue-tint` and set its text in `--status-blue-text`. Down opens the menu from the button; Esc closes it and puts focus back on the button; Tab closes it.
- **Look: the section "+".** 24 by 24 px under the section's grip, in the left margin, outside the section's own box. `--text-muted` icon; hover `--grey-100` with `--text`; focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring drawn inside the button (offset -2 px). Faint at rest (opacity `--sh-rest`: .3 in light, .75 in dark) and clear while the section is hovered, has focus or has its menu open; always clear on a touch screen. Its name is "Add a template below" and the section's title.
- **Look: the "Add a section" bar.** Only when Edit shows a report with no sections: full width, 28 px high at least, a dashed 1 px `--grey-300` edge, `--radius-sm`, 13 px `--text-muted` words after a plus; hover turns the edge `--grey-500` and the text `--text`; focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring.
- **States.** Rest, hover, focus, open (the button reports `aria-expanded`). Reduced motion removes the grow and the colour change from the Add button, and the fade from the section "+". A published file is read only and has none of these.
- **What a click does on main.** The Add button creates nothing yet: it says in the status line under the title "Add <kind> is coming with a later release. Nothing was added." (the kind is the word picked). The section "+" and the bar open the Add a section box (described with the dialogs).
- **900 px.** The Add button keeps its 32 px square and grows only on hover or focus. The section "+" moves inside the section's own padding (32 px on the left, the "+" 4 px from the edge) so it never hangs outside the card.
- **Measured** on main at abc1bc8d (both widths, light and dark): Work items in Edit at rest, with the Add button grown by keyboard focus, and with its menu open, and the Dashboard in Edit, and the empty Dashboard in Edit with its bar: contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, overflow 0, no sideways page scroll. Worked by hand from the live values (the script does not read non-text contrast): text on the grown button is white on `--brand-blue`, 6.3 to 1 in light.
- **Below 3 to 1 (worked by hand, light only).** The section "+" at rest is about 1.6 to 1 against the page (about 7.4 to 1 once clear); the dashed edge of the "Add a section" bar is about 1.6 to 1.
- **Not yet checked.** Reduced motion on the grow, the "+" on a touch screen, the menu with a screen reader, and the dark values of the two below-3-to-1 items.
- **Open points.** The section "+" is easy to miss at rest. The styled tip repeats the word the grown button already shows, and it falls over the layout switch below the button. In Edit a tile's grip can overlap the end of a long tile title (measured: the grip starts 26 px inside the title of the "High risks and issues" tile at 1400 px).

## 12. Editable cells in the Work items table (Edit)
The Owner, Due, Priority and Status cells of the table, which change in place in Edit, against the same cells in View.
- **View, for comparison.** `table.items`: fixed layout, 14 px text, header cells 13 px capitals in `--text-muted`, a `--grey-100` line between rows, the Item column takes what the others leave (Type 116, Owner 170, Due 134, Priority 104, Status 152 px). A row opens the item's peek: hover `--grey-50`, focus a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring inside the row. The four cells are plain text.
- **Look in Edit.** The cell's content becomes a `.cell-pick` button: at least 28 px high, padding 3 by 6 px with the same negative margin, so the table does not move when Edit is switched on. At rest it has a quiet 1 px `--grey-300` line along its foot and a 13 px caret "▾" in `--text-muted` after the value. Hover, and while its picker is open: fill `--grey-100` and a 1 px `--grey-300` ring all round. Focus is a 2 px `--brand-blue` ring, offset 1 px.
- **One line.** Due, Priority and Status never wrap, so the caret sits after the value. Owner wraps only between words, and the caret never drops to a line of its own. Measured sizes at 1400 px: Owner 150 by 48 (name on two lines), Due 114 by 47 (date, with the days line under it), Priority 73 by 28, Status 93 by 30. The Status cell shows the item's own status as a pill (Open, Delayed, Closed and so on), the same word its picker offers.
- **Cells that are not pickers.** A risk or issue's Priority is set from impact and likelihood: plain text with the tip "Set from impact and likelihood". A decision has no priority and shows a dash.
- **The picker.** One panel at a time, fixed under the cell (above it when there is no room, and moved up to fit the window): `--surface`, `--border` edge, `--radius`, `--shadow`, 14 px text, 180 px at least and 320 px at most. Status and Priority are a menu of radio items with the current one ticked (✓ in `--status-blue-text`) and a small-print note where there is one (Made on a pending decision says "Can't be undone"). Owner is 260 px wide: a 32 px text box, up to 8 matching names with a 24 px initials badge, and the line "Press Enter to keep what you typed." Due is 260 px wide: a month with ‹ and › buttons, a seven-column grid of 30 px day buttons (today has a 1 px `--brand-blue` ring; the chosen day is `--brand-blue` with `--text-on-brand`, heavier and underlined, so it is not colour alone), then quick choices of 28 px rows.
- **Keys.** The table is one tab stop; Up and Down move between rows (in Edit from a cell to the same cell below), Right and Left move across the cells. Enter, Space or Alt+Down opens the picker, and a pick is one change with Undo in the message. Esc, a click away or Tab closes it and leaves the value as it was; Esc puts focus back on the cell.
- **Names for a screen reader.** Each cell reads as for example "Status of <id>: Open. Change". Status and Priority announce a menu; Owner and Due announce a dialog.
- **900 px.** The table stays a table. The fixed columns take 676 px, so the Item column falls to 158 px and titles run to three or four lines; Owner wraps to two. It does not scroll sideways.
- **Measured** on main at abc1bc8d (both widths, light and dark): Work items in Edit at rest, and with each of the four pickers open: contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, overflow 0, no sideways page scroll. Worked by hand: the caret is about 7.4 to 1 in light; the quiet foot line is about 1.6 to 1.
- **Not yet checked.** A pick made through to its Undo message, the problem line in the Owner panel, a decision's Made note on screen, reduced motion, a screen reader on the pickers, and the foot line in dark.
- **Open points.** The foot line alone is below 3 to 1, so the caret is the real cue. At 900 px the Item column is narrow; the table has no rule yet for what gives way first.

## 13. Empty states
What each page says, and shows, when there is nothing to show. Each is one quiet sentence in `--text-muted` under the page title, with no picture and no icon; where there is a next step, the sentence names it.
- **Dashboard.** View: "This report has no sections yet. Switch to Edit to add one." A published file says "This report has no sections." If sections exist and every one is hidden: "Every section of this report is hidden. Switch to Edit to show them." Edit: "This report has no sections yet. Add the first one below." with the "Add a section" bar under it (see 11).
- **Work items.** No items at all: "This project has no work items yet. Use Add item to add the first." (a published file: "This report has no work items."); the filter bar is not drawn. Items exist but the filters hide them: a centred "No items match these filters." with a "Clear all" button.
- **Board.** An empty column says "Nothing here" (13 px); a column that cannot take a card says "Can't drop here" ("Can't drop here. Use This week, Next week or Later." for the overdue column). In View a line beside the full-screen button says "Switch to Edit to move cards."
- **Milestones.** "No milestones are shown yet." It names no next step.
- **Use cases.** "No use cases yet." A use case with no steps says "No open steps" on its row and "No open next steps." when opened; a step with no owner reads "No owner".
- **Settings.** The title frame is the one centred empty frame: the title and "Settings are not ready yet." The blocks under it have their own: "No client folder yet." with a Choose folder button, and "There are no backups to show yet."
- **No project.** "No project open" with "There are no projects yet." and the line that says how to start with made-up projects, or "Choose a project." with the list, or "Looking for projects.", or the reason with a Try again button.
- **Look.** The sentence is `--text-muted` body text (15 px), left-aligned in the page's frame; only the Settings title frame, the no-project screen and the no-match line are centred. A person's own look may set a still picture above the centred frames (`--art-home`); it is flat and empty by default.
- **900 px.** The same words, one column, no sideways scroll.
- **Measured** on main at abc1bc8d (both widths, light and dark): the Dashboard empty in View and in Edit, Work items with no items in Edit, Milestones empty, Settings, and Work items with a filter that matches nothing: contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, overflow 0, no sideways page scroll. For the empty screens the page's own data request was answered with empty lists in the browser, so the page drew what it draws for a project with none.
- **Not yet checked.** An empty board column in the live page (the demo's columns are never empty), the no-project screens, whether any of these is announced to a screen reader (only the Work items note line is a status region), and the published page's wording on a real file.
- **Open points.** Milestones and Use cases name no next step. The Work items sentence tells the person to use Add item, which creates nothing yet (see 11). The Settings title says "Settings are not ready yet." above settings that work. The centred and left-aligned empty frames do not follow one rule.

## 14. Keyboard shortcuts and key hints
The shortcuts table in Help, the single-key switch in Settings, and the key letters in tooltips.
- **The table.** Help, Reference, "Keyboard shortcuts": two columns, Keys and Does, 866 px wide at 1400 px, a `--grey-200` line under the heading and a `--grey-100` line between rows, text 14 px. A key is a `kbd` chip: 13 px, a 1 px `--grey-300` edge with a 2 px foot, 4 px radius, 3 by 5 px padding, at least 1.6 em wide, `--surface` fill, `--text`. Arrows are drawn as ← → ↑ ↓, the platform's own key (⌘ or Ctrl) stands for Mod, and a chord is joined by a plain "+". The same chip sits in Help's opening line ("Press ? on any page...").
- **What is listed on main.** Six rows, the same in View and in Edit: ? (Help, from any page, when not typing in a box); ↑ ↓ Enter (move and choose in any menu, Esc closes); V (switch to View); E (switch to Edit); Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ (move a section, in Edit, on its grip); Home and End (move between Help's tabs). The other rows in the source are held back until their parts are on the page.
- **The single-key switch.** Settings, Personal, Keys: a check box "Single-key shortcuts", on by default, remembered in this browser on this computer, applied at once with no reload. When it is off V and E do nothing, and the tooltips and the key-shortcut names of the View and Edit buttons lose their letters (checked live). Arrows, Enter, Esc, Space and Ctrl or Cmd combinations still work.
- **When V and E act.** On the Dashboard of a page clark serves, with a plain key only. Not with a modifier, a held key or an input method composing; not when focus is in a field, the editor, a menu or a dialog; not while the peek or any dialog or menu is open. A key for the mode already shown does nothing. Checked live: E switches to Edit and V back to View.
- **Key hints.** The View and Edit buttons have the tooltips "View (V)" and "Edit (E)" and name the key to a screen reader. A section grip and a tile grip name their Alt-arrow keys to a screen reader but show no hint. The board's full-screen button says "View full screen" and, when on, "Leave full screen (Esc)".
- **900 px.** The table keeps its two columns and does not scroll sideways.
- **Measured** on main at abc1bc8d (both widths, light and dark): Help open on the Reference tab over the Dashboard, in View and in Edit: contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, overflow 0, no sideways page scroll.
- **Not yet checked.** The Mod rows (none is listed on main today), the keys with a screen reader or voice control, the chips on a Windows computer, and the held rows once they return.
- **Open points.** The tooltips "View (V)" and "Edit (E)" show on every page, but the keys act on the Dashboard only. The switch is in Settings and the table says so only in the V and E rows. The V and E rows repeat the same long sentence.

## 15. Tooltips
Two kinds are in use, and they look different.
- **The styled tip (`data-tip`).** A one-line label drawn under its control on hover and on keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`), with no delay and no fade. It sits 6 px below the control, aligned to its right edge, `z-index` 30; padding 4 by 8 px, `--radius-sm`, `--brand-navy` fill, `--white` text, 13 px on a 1.4 line, no wrapping. It cannot be pointed at (no pointer events) and goes when the pointer or focus leaves. Contrast, worked by hand from the live fill: about 14.2 to 1 in light and 16.9 to 1 in dark.
- **Where it is used.** The Add button ("Add item" and the others), the profile button ("Your menu: Publish, Preview, Export, Settings"), the Table and Board buttons, and the board's full-screen button.
- **The browser's own tip (`title`).** Drawn by the browser, so clark does not style it. Used for the View and Edit buttons (with the key letter), the section grip ("Drag to move · click for options"), the section "+" ("Add a template below"), a tile's eye ("Hide from the published report", or "Hidden from the published report. Show it."), a person's initials (the full name), the Timeline and Calendar buttons, a milestone's marker (name, date, state), a moved milestone's ghost ("Expected" and the date), the Progress bar, and a risk's Priority ("Set from impact and likelihood").
- **Rule in the code.** A control that has a tip also has its own accessible name (an `aria-label`), so no one depends on the tip to learn what it does.
- **900 px.** The tip is not resized; the profile tip (273 px wide, right-aligned) stays inside the window.
- **Measured** on main at abc1bc8d (both widths, light and dark): Work items with the Table button focused by keyboard: contrast failures 0, text under 12 px 0, controls under 24 px 0, overflow 0, no sideways page scroll. The script cannot read the tip's own text, so its contrast was worked by hand.
- **Fails on the live page.** The tips of the Table and Board buttons are not drawn: the group they sit in clips what lies outside it (`.seg` has `overflow: hidden`, and the tip is below the buttons). The tip is in the computed style but not on screen, and these two buttons have no `title` to fall back on.
- **Also found.** The Add and profile buttons carry both a `title` and a `data-tip`, so a browser may show both. In dark the tip has no edge and its fill is within a shade of the page. The tip under the Add button covers the layout switch below it. No code closes a tip with Esc, and a tip cannot be moved onto.
- **Not yet checked.** How the browser's own tip looks and how long it waits, whether a screen reader reads a `title` as well as the name, the tip beside the edge of the window for a short control on the left, and touch (there is no hover).
- **Open points.** One kind would be easier to keep: either every hint is styled and clipped nowhere, or all are the browser's. A milestone's name hidden at 900 px in the Calendar has no tip (see 3). The Forecast state of a milestone is not written up here: it shows as "Forecast, not agreed" in the list, as "Forecast" in a marker's title and as a hollow ring in the Movement bar, and needs its own entry.
