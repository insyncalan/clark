Settings

What each card on this page is for.

  • Personal. Your name, initials and how dates and numbers show.
  • People. Who is on the team and what each person can do.
  • Freshness. How old a status can be before clark marks it as stale.
  • Publishing. The cover, the footer and the sections a client copy leaves out.
  • Published reports. The finished copies you have sent, and who has them.
  • Backups. Where copies of your data are kept and when the last one ran.
  • Data. Bring in a workbook, or send your data out.
  • Update. Check for a new version and install it.
